# Commission of Inquiry (Yendi Events) (Amendment) Instrument, 2002 C.I. 37

C.I. 37

COMMISSION OF INQUIRY (YENDI EVENTS) (AMENDMENT) INSTRUMENT, The Commission of Inquiry (Yendi Events) Instrument, 2002 (C.I. 36) is amended by the substitution of subparagraph (d) of paragraph 2 of the following: "(d) To submit, not later than the 31st of August, 2002, its report to the President giving reasons for its findings and recommendations. " Date of Gazette notification: 5th July, 2002.1
