# Internal Revenue (Registration of Business) (Amendment) Act, 2008 ( Act 777) (Revised Edition)

INTERNAL REVENUE (REGISTRATION OF BUSINESS) (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2008 ( ACT 777)

ACT 777

THE SEVEN HUNDRED AND SEVENTY-SEVENTH ACT OF THE PARLIAMENT OF THE REPUBLIC OF GHANA ENTITLED INTERNAL REVENUE (REGISTRATION OF BUSINESS) (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2008 AN ACT to amend the Internal Revenue (Registration of Business) Act, 2005 (Act 684) and to provide for related matters. DATE OF ASSENT: 6th January, 2009. ENACTED by the President and Parliament: Schedule of Act 684 amended The Internal Revenue (Registration of Business) Act, 2005 (Act 684) is amended in the Schedule by the substitution for ¢100,000.00'' ¢75,000.00 and ¢50,000.00 of 'GH¢10.00', GH¢7.50GP" and GH¢5.00" respectively. Date of Gazette Notification: 9th January, 2009.
