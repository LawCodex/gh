Part I—Enactments repealed Part II—Enactments revoked

ACT 543

THE FIVE HUNDRED AND FORTY - THIRD ACT OF THE PARLIAMENT OF THE REPUBLIC OF GHANA ENTITLED THE STATUTE LAW REVISION ACT, 1997 AN ACT to repeal certain enactments relating to confiscation and freezing of assets. DATE OF ASSENT: 26TH JANUARY, 1998 BE IT ENACTED BY Parliament as follows—

### Section 1 — Repeal of Enactments Specified in Part I of the Schedule

The enactments specified in Part I of the Schedule to this Act are hereby repealed.

### Section 2 — Revocation of Enactments Specified in Part II of the Schedule

The enactments specified in Part II of the Schedule to this Act are hereby revoked.

### Section 3 — Defreezing of Bank Accounts

Any bank account frozen by or under any of the enactments specified in the schedule to this Act is hereby defrozen.
