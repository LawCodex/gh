NATIONAL COMMISSION ON CULTURE ACT, 1990! _

AN ACT to establish a National Commission on Culture in consonance with article 39 if of the Constitution and to provide for related matters. National Commission on Culture

## Section 1

Establishment of National Commission

(1) In consonance with article 39 of the Constitution, there is hereby established a National Commission.

(2) The Commission is a public corporation as defined in article 295 of the Constitution. ‘

## Section 2

Functions of the Commission

The functions of the Commission are to translate the objectives, programmes and spirit of article 39 of the Constitution into reality by

(a) initiating policies and programmes for the dissemination and propagation of ideas for the promotion of national pride, solidarity and consciousness;

(b) prompting the evolution of an integrated national culture thus creating a district Ghanaian personality to be reflected in African and world affairs;

(c) supervising the implementation of programmes for the preservation, promotion and presentation of Ghanaian culture;

(d) seeking to promote an educational system that motivates and stimulates creativity and draws largely on Ghanaian traditions and values;

(e) establishing a code of behaviour compatible with the Ghanaian tradition of humanism and a disciplined and moral society;

(f) promoting national self-reliance and self-sufficiency, and reflect Ghanaian cultural heritage and national aspirations in the process of industrialisation;

(g) working in collaboration with other agencies to promote the broad aims and objectives of the Commission;

(h) establishing administrative structures and recommend the provision of funds for the implementation of cultural programmes;

(i) organising an annual congress of Ghanaian artists and cultural workers;

(j) performing any other functions prescribed by the President.

Appointment of committees The Board may, for the performance of the functions of the Commission appoint committees comprising of members of the Board or non-members or both and may assign to the committees a function of the Commission as determined by the Board.

## Section 8

Disclosure of interest

(1) A member of the Board who has an interest whether directly or indirectly, in a matter that is a subject for the consideration of the Board shall disclose in writing to the Board the nature of the interest and shall not participate in a discussion or decision of the Board relating to that matter.

(2) A member who fails to disclose interest under subsection (1) shall cease to be a member of the Board. Regional Centres 9, Establishment of regional centres A centre for national culture shall be established in each Region.
