# Ministries, Departments and Agencies (Retention of Funds) Act, 2007 (Act 735)

ACT 735

THE SEVEN HUNDRED AND THIRTY-FIFTH ACT OF THE PARLIAMENT OF THE REPUBLIC OF GHANA ENTITLED MINISTRIES, DEPARTMENTS AND AGENCIES (RETENTION OF FUNDS) ACT, 2007 AN ACT to authorise the generation and retention by Ministries, Departments and Agencies of a percentage of the moneys they realise in the course of the performance of their functions and to provide for related matters. DATE OF ASSENT: 18th May, 2007. ENACTED by the President and Parliament:

## Section 1 — Retention of percentage of collections

(1) The preparation and submission of estimates and the reporting and accounting for them are subject to the Financial Administration Act, 2003 (Act 654).

(2) The retention of moneys is subject to the Financial Administration Act 2003 (Act 654).

(3) Ministries, Departments and Agencies specified in the first column of the First Schedule shall retain out of the revenues and other moneys realised or received by them in the performance of their functions, the percentage specified in the second column of the First Schedule.

## Section 2 — Existing enactments amended

The enactments specified in the first column of the Second Schedule are hereby amended as stipulated in the second column.

## Section 3 — Ministries, Departments and Agencies empowered to internally generate and retain

funds A Ministry, Department and Agency specified in the first column of the Third Schedule may,

(a) raise or receive moneys in respect of the performance of its functions, and

(b) retain out of moneys raised or received, the percentage stipulated in the second column of the Schedule.

## Section 4 — Accounts and audit

The provisions of article 187 of the Constitution which relate to the Auditor-General shall apply to the moneys retained under this Act.

## Section 5 — Utilisation of internally generated Funds

Despite any other provision in any enactment to the contrary

(a) Internally Generated funds can only be utilised when the activities on which the expenditure will be incurred have been programmed and approved in that Ministry's, Department's and Agency's expenditure budget by Parliament;

(b) Ministries, Departments and Agencies which have approval to retain and utilise internally generated funds can only incur expenditure funded by internally generated funds for a particular month if the records of collection for the previous month, bank lodgments, and expenditure returns for the previous month have been submitted to the Ministry of Finance;

(c) Internally Generated Funds shall not be used for the payment of salaries, staff benefits and other allowances except where the allowances are directly related to the provision of services that will lead to increased revenue.

## Section 6 — Amendment of Schedules

The Minister for Finance may by legislative instrument amend the Schedules to this Act.
