# Declaration of President-Elect Instrument, 2000.C.I.32 (Revised Edition)

Declaration of President-Elect Instrument, 2000

DECLARATION OF PRESIDENT-ELECT INSTRUMENT, 2000 |

[CL 32]

toy [(Article 63(9)) (30th December, 2000)| |

MR. JOHN AGYEKUM KUFUOR, the New Patriotic Party (NPP) presidential |

candidate having, in the run-off to the Presidential elections held on the 28th day of De-

cember, 2000, pursuant to article 63 (4) and (5) of the Constitution, obtained more than

fifty percent of the valid votes cast and is hereby declared the President-elect of the Re-

public of Ghana.

[Date of Gazette notification: 5th January, 2001] | >
