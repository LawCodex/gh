Establishment

## Section 1

Establishment of National Commission for Civic Education _

(1) In accordance with article 231 of the Constitution there is hereby established a National Commission for Civic Education.

(2) The Commission is a public corporation as defined in article 295 of the Constitution.

## Section 2

The functions of the Commission . The functions of the Commission are,

(a) to create and sustain within the society the awareness of the principles and objectives of the Constitution as the fundamental law;

(b) to educate and encourage the public to defend the Constitution at all times, against all forms of abuse and violation;

(c) to formulate for the consideration of Government, programmes at the national, regional and district level to realise the objectives of the Constitution;

(d) to formulate, implement and oversee programmes intended to inculcate in citizens an awareness of their civic responsibilities and an appreciation of the rights and obligations of citizens as a free people; and

(e) to assess for the information of the Government, the limit to the achievement of true democracy caused by the inequalities between different levels of the population and to make recommendations to redress these inequalities.

## Section 3

Independence of Commission As provided in article 234 of the Constitution, except as otherwise provided in the Constitution or in any other law which is not inconsistent with the Constitution, the Commission is not subject to the direction or control of a person or an authority in the performance of its functions. BG 7. Terms and conditions of service of members of the Board

(1) The chairman of the Commission shall enjoy the same terms and conditions of service as a Justice of the Court of Appeal and a deputy chairman shall enjoy the same terms and conditions of service as a Justice of the High Court.

Filling vacancies

(1) Where a member of the Board, other than the chairman, is incapacitated by illness or any other sufficient cause from performing the functions of a member of the Board, the President may, acting on the advice of the Council of State appoint another person to perform those functions until the member is able to resume the performance of those functions.

(2) Where the chairman of the Board dies, resigns, is removed from office or is ab- | sent from the Republic for a continuous period exceeding three months or is by reason of : illness unable to perform the functions of the chairman, one of the deputy chairmen as | directed by the President shall act as chairman, until the chairman is able to perform the | functions as chairman or until a new chairman is appointed. :

## Section 11

Meetings of the Commission

(1) The Commission shall meet at least once in every month for the despatch of business at a time and place determined by the chairman.

(2) The quorum at a meeting of the Commission is four of the members including the chairman or a deputy chairman.

(3) A notice of four clear days for every meeting of the Commission shall be given to ; a member, ;

(4) The chairman shall preside at the meetings of the Commission but in the absence of the chairman, one of the deputy chairmen shall preside.

(5) The validity of the proceedings of the Commission shall not be affected by a vacancy in its membership or a defect in the appointment of a member.

(6) Questions before the Commission shall be decided by a majority of the members present and voting and where there is equality of votes the chairman shall have a second or casting vote.

(7) The chairman shall summon a special meeting of the Commission within fourteen days of the receipt of a written request signed by not less than four members of the Board.

(8) Except as otherwise provided in this Act, the Commission shall regulate the procedure at its meetings.
