Change-over to right-hand traffic

(1) As from the prescribed date,

(a) a motor vehicle shall in approaching and passing traffic coming from the opposite direction, be driven on the right side of the highway;

(b) in respect of any highway which is open to movement of traffic in both directions, a motor vehicle when overtaking traffic proceeding in the same direction, shall pass the traffic on the left side, | and the provisions of an enactment relating to road traffic shall be construed subject to this Act.

(2) In this Act the “prescribed date” means the 4th day of August, 1974 or any other date that the Minister may by Legislative Instrument prescribe. 2. ## Section 7

Members not personally liable

A member of a committee shall not be personally liable for an act or an omission done or made in good faith while engaged on the business of the committee in respect of the change-over to right-hand traffic.
