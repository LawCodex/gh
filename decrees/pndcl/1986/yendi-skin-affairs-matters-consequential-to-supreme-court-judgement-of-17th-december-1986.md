AN ACT to give effect to a judgment of the Supreme Court and to provide for related matters.

## Section 1

Enskinment of Adam Mahama and Yakubu Abdu-lai declared null The nomination, selection and enskinment of

(a) Adam Mahama as Karaga Na,

(b) Yakubu Abdu-lai as Savelugu-Na, of the Yendi Traditional Area is hereby declared null for all purposes, whether under customary law or otherwise. : 1. ## Section 2

Adam Mahama and Yakubu Abdu-lai not te purport to be chiefs

Adam Mahama and Yakubu Abdu-lai are hereby prohibited from exercising or pur- ‘S porting to exercise the functions of Karaga-Na and Savelugu-Na respectively of the Yendi Traditional Area, whether under customary law or otherwise.

## Section 3

Prohibition of Adam Mahama and Yakubu Abdu-lai from Karaga and Savelugu respectively

(1) Unless the President otherwise directs,

(a) Adam Mahama is hereby prohibited from residing, entering or remaining in the town of Karaga or from residing, entering or remaining in any place ~, within a radius of 86 kilometres from Karaga;

(b) Yakubu Abdu-lai is hereby prohibited from entering, residing or remaining in the town of Savelugu or residing, entering or remaining in any place within a radius of 24 kilometres from Savelugu.

(2) Where Adam Mahama or Yakubu Abdu-lai without lawful excuse, the proof of which lies on him, contravenes subsections (1) he commits an offence and is liable on summary conviction to a term of imprisonment of not less than two years and not more than ten years, without the option of a fine.

## Section 4

Authorisation to take possession of skin property

(1) Until the time that a new Karaga-Na and a new Savelugu-Na are enskinned for Karaga and Savelugu respectively and the President in writing directs the restoration of the skin properties, the Regional Minister is hereby authorised to take possession of the Karaga and Savelugu skin properties.

(2) The Regional Minister shall as soon as practicable after taking possession of the skin properties prepare an inventory of those properties accompanied by a certificate of correctness signed

(a) jointly by the Regional Minister and Adam Mahama in respect of the Karaga skin; and

(b) jointly by the Regional Minister and Yakubu Abdu-lai in respect of the Savelugu skin.

(3) The Regional Minister shall forward a copy of the inventory together with the signed certificate of correctness to the Minister responsible for chieftaincy matters.

(4) A person who obstructs the Regional Minister from taking possession of the — Karaga and Savelugu skin properties commits an offence and is liable on summary con- } viction to a term of imprisonment of not less than two years and not more than five years without the option of a fine.
