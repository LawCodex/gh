Judgment and order of Court set aside

Despite a law to the contrary, a judgment or an order of a Court in respect of a cause oe or matter relating to or connected with the subject matter of the illegal seizure of Ivorian a or Ghanaian farms by some chiefs in Brong Ahafo is set aside.
