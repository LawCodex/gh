SIMON DIEDONG DOMBO UNIVERSITY OF BUSINESS AND INTEGRATED DEVELOPMENT STUDIES (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2025

AN ACT to amend the Simon Diedong Dombo University of Business and Integrated Development Studies Act, 2019 (Act 1001) to change the name ofthe University to the University ofBusiness and Integrated Development Studies, Wa and to provide for related matters.

Date OF ASSENT: 24hDecember, 2025. PASSED by Parliament and assented to by the President:
