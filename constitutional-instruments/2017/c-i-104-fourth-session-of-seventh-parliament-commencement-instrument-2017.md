# Fourth Session of Seventh Parliament (Commencement) Instrument, 2017 C. I. 104

C. I. 104 FOURTH SESSION OF SEVENTH PARLIAMENT

(COMMENCEMENT)INSTRUMENT, 2017

ICNOW YE ALL MEN that in exercise of the powers conferred on the Speaker by clause (1) of article 112 of the Constitution, I, Rt. Hon.

Prof. Michael Aaron Oquaye, Speaker of Parliament of Ghana, by this Constitutional Instrument, appoint Parliament House, Accra, to be the place, and ten o'clock in the forenoon of Saturday, 7th January, 2017 to be the time at which the Fourth Session of the Seventh Parliament of

the Fourth Repubhc of Ghana shall commence.

Given under my hand this 7th January, 2017.

RT. Hon.EDWARD K.DOE ADJAHO

Speaker ofParliament

Date ofGazette notification: 8th May,2017.
