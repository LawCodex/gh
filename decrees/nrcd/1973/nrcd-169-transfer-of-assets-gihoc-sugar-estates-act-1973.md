Commencement. oN SCHEDULE N.R.C.D. 169 TRANSFER OF ASSETS (GIHOC SUGAR ESTATES) ACT, 1973' | AN ACT to provide for the transfer of certain assets and for related matters. 1. Assets and liabilities transferred to Ghana Sugar Estates Limited

(1) With effect from the commencement of this Act, and despite anything to the contrary, there shall be transferred to the Ghana Sugar Estates Limited the assets and current liabilities, excluding overdrafts of the Ghana Industrial Holding Corporation relating to | the sugar business operated by the Sugar Division of that Corporation at Komenda and Asutuare respectively.

(2) Without prejudice to subsection (1), there is hereby vested in the Ghana Sugar Estates Limited, free from all encumbrances, the two sugar-cane estates which form part of the assets referred to in subsection (1) described in the Schedule for the terms respectively specified.

(3) The Ghana Sugar Estates Limited shall have full, exclusive and uninterrupted possession of the sugar-cane estate at Komenda during the term of years, and is entitled to continue the business of sugar-cane production and processing. 2. The Minister may take incidental measures The Minister responsible for Industries shall take the measures that are reasonably in- | cidental to the transfer, and accordingly for that purpose the Minister may give necessary ; directions, and a person concerned to shall comply with those directions. i. The Act was issued as the Transfer of Assets (GIHOC Sugar Estates) Decree, 1973 (N.R.C.D. 169), made on the 24th day of March, 1973 and notified in the Gazette on 30th March, 1973. The preamble to the Decree reads, “WHEREAS in furtherance of the general economic policy of the National Redemption Council of promoting self-reliance, the Government is desirous of rehabilitating the sugar industry in Ghana, and has accordingly requested the International Development Association for assistance in financing the rehabilitation of the sugar estates and factories at Komenda and Asutuare respectively; \ AND WHEARES for this purpose a company known as the Ghana Sugar Estates Limited has been es- } tablished to take over all the assets and current liabilities (excluding overdraft) of the Ghana Industrial Holding Corporation relating to the said sugar estates and factories at Komenda and Asutuare respectively.” Vi — 3151 [ssue 1]
