## Section 2

Specific concessions vested in the Corporation Without prejudice to the general effect of section 1, the concessions specified in the second column of the Schedule are hereby vested in the Corporation for the unexpired ™y residue of the several terms of years for which those concessions were originally respecioe tively granted to the contracting party, together with

(a) the rights, easements, profits, advantages and appurtenances to the concessions belonging or appertaining to the concessions or with the same usually held, occupied and enjoyed or reputed to belong or to be appurtenant to the concessions, and

(b) the estate rights, title, interest, claim and demand of the contracting party, in, to and on the concessions and every part of the concession.

## Section 3

Payment of rents, royalties The vesting in the Corporation of the properties specified under sections 1 and 2 are where necessary, subject to the payment of the several rents and royalties, and performance and observance of the covenants, conditions, stipulations and encumbrances under which those properties were held by the contracting party.

## Section 4

Persons possessing certain property

(1) A person who is in possession or control of property belonging to the contracting party shall notify in writing, within thirty days after the date on which this Act is published in the Gazette, or within thirty days after the property came into that person’s possession or control, the managing director of the Corporation, to the best of that person’s knowledge

(a) the nature of the property, and

(b) the extent of the property.

(2) Except as the managing director may otherwise direct, a person shall not dispose of a property referred to in subsection (1).

## Section 5

Penalty fy A person who contravenes a provision of section 4 commits an offence and is liable “ on conviction to a fine not exceeding five hundred penalty units or to a term of imprisonment not exceeding two years, or to both the fine and the imprisonment.
