# Court of Appeal (Commission of Inquiry) Appeals Rules, 2010 C.I.66

C. 1. 66 @ COURT OF APPEAL (COMMISSIONS OF INQUIRY) APPEALS RULES, 2010 IN THE EXERCISE of the powers conferred on the Rules of Court Committee by clause (2) of article 281 of the Constitution, these Rules are hereby made this 12th day of November, 2009. > a) Appeals from Commissions of Inquiry | 1. The Court of Appeals Rules, 1997 (C. I. 19) relating to Civil Appeals shall, with the necessary modifications which the Court considers appropriate, apply to an appeal brought under clause (2) of article 280 of the Constitution in respect of the adverse findings of a Commission of Inquiry. ppeer- 2 wt _ pe
