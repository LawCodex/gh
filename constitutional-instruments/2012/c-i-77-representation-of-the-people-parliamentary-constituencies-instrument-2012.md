CONSTITUENCIES) INSTRUMENT, 2012 IN exercise of the powers conferred on the Electoral Commission by article 47 clauses (1) and (5) and article 51 of the Constitution, these Regulations are made this 14th day of August, 2012. | Constituencies for election to Parliament ‘a 1. (1) Ghana is divided into two hundred and seventy five Constituencies for the purposes of election of members of the Parliament which will hold its first sitting in January, 2013, and for subsequent elections.

(2) Each Constituency shall be represented by one member in Parliament. Area of constituencies 2. The electoral areas specified in the third column of the Schedule are hereby determined as the electoral areas of the constituencies specified in the first column of the Schedule and the constituency headquarters are as specified in the second column of the Schedule.
