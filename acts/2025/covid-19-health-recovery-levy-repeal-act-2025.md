REPUBLIC OF GHANA

The One thousand one hundred and fiftieth

ACT OF THE PARLIAMENT OF THE REPUBLIC OF GHANA ENTITLED

COVID-19 HEALTH RECOVERY LEVY (REPEAL) ACT, 2025 AN ACT to repeal the COVID-19 Health Recovery Levy Act, 2021 (Act 1068) and provide for related matters. DATE OF ASSENT: 1(F December, 2025. PASSED by Parliament and assented to by the President:

Act 1068 repealed 1. The COVID-19 Health Recovery Levy Act, 2021 (Act 1068) is repealed.

Savings 2. Despite the repeal of Act 1068, a right, a liability or an obligation in existence immediately before the repeal of Act 1068 shall continue in existence until the right, liability or obligation is exercised or terminated.
