VIRTUAL ASSET SERVICE PROVIDERS ACT, 2025 AN ACT to regulate the provision of a virtual asset service in or from the country and to provide for related matters. DATE OF ASSENT: 24" December, 2025. PASSED by Parliament and assented to by the President: Preliminary Provision Application of this Act

## Section 1

(1) This Act applies to a virtual asset service provider who provides a virtual asset service in or from the country.

(2) This Act does not apply to

(a) a digital representation of a fiat currency, securities or any other financial asset that is regulated under an existing law;

(b) a digital currency issued by the Bank of Ghana or the central bank of any other country;

(c) a virtual service token; or

(d) any other digital representation of a value or right that may be determined by the relevant regulatory authority.

(3) Where there is a conflict or inconsistency between this Act and any other enactment in respect of a virtual asset service, the provisions of this Act shall prevail.

## Section 6

(1) A person shall not provide or purport to provide a virtual asset service in or from the country unless the person

(a) has registered in accordance with this Act;

(b) holds a virtual asset service licence issued under this Act; or

(c) is a supervised person that has been granted a waiver by the relevant regulatory authority under section 51.

(2) For the purposes of subsection (1), a person purports to provide a virtual asset service if the person

(a) uses a word in the description or title under which the person carries on business that connotes a virtual asset service;

(b) makes a representation in a document or in any other manner that the person is providing a virtual asset service; or

(c) otherwise holds out as providing a virtual asset service.

(3) A person qualifies to apply for registration, a virtual asset service licence or a waiver under this Act if that person is

(a) a company incorporated under the Companies Act, 2019 (Act 992);

(b) a partnership established under the Incorporated Private Partnership Act, 1962 (Act 152); or (¢) anon-Ghanaian company, as defined under the Companies Act, 2019 (Act 992).

(4) For the purposes of paragraph (6) of subsection (3), anon-Ghanaian company shall comply with the provisions applicable to an external company under Chapter Five of the Companies Act, 2019 (Act 992) as if the non-Ghanaian company were an external company.

(S) An individual shall not provide or purport to provide in or from the country a virtual asset service

(a) as a business; or

(6) in the course of business.

(6) A person who is not registered, issued with a licence or granted a waiver under this Act, including an applicant for registration, a licence or a waiver shall not state, imply or convey that the person is regulated or authorised by the relevant regulatory authority to provide a virtual asset service.

(7) A person who contravenes subsection (1), (5) or (6) commits an offence and is liable on summary conviction to a fine of not less than twenty thousand penalty units and not more than one million penalty units or to a term of imprisonment of not less than six months and not more than ten years or to both.
