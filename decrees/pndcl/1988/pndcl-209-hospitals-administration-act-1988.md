## Section 5

Meetings of a Board

(1) A Board shall meet at least once every month at the times and places determined by the chairman.

(2) The chairman shall preside at the meetings of the Board and in the absence of the chairman, a member of the Board elected by the members present shall preside.

(3) The quorum at a meeting of a Board is five.

(4) A Council may co-opt a person to act as an advisor at any of its meetings but a person so co-opted shall not vote on a matter for decision before the Board.

(5) A member of a Board who has an interest, direct or indirect, in a company or an undertaking which has financial concern in a matter that is a subject for the consideration of the Council shall disclose in writing to the Board the nature of the interest and shall not participate in a discussion or decision of the Board relating to that matter.

(6) A member of a Board who fails to disclose interest under subsection (5) ceases to be a member.

(7) Subject to this Act, a Board shall regulate its own procedures.
