## Section 1

Registration of newspapers and publications | : (1) A newspaper or a publication which is printed in the Republic of Ghana, shall be registered with the Commission by its proprietor within thirty days of first publication. |

(2) A person or representative of a newspaper or publication printed outside the Re- | public who imports the newspaper or publication into the Republic shall register the newspaper or publication with the Commission within thirty days of the first importation.

## Section 2

Ferm of application A person registering a newspaper or a publication under regulation 1 shall use the Form 1 set out in the Schedule.

## Section 3

Registration

(1) The person registering shail pay a registration fee of ¢100,000 or any other sum : of money determined by the Commission. |

(2) The registration form shall be accompanied with a detailed curriculum vitae of the editor, publisher and proprietor of the newspaper. H |

## Section 4

Certificate of registration i On registration of a newspaper or publication under this Instrument, the Commission | shall within thirty days of the registration issue to the person registering, a certificate of ; registration as specified in the Form 2 set out in the Schedule. :

Transitional provisions

## Section 8

Offence |

A person who fails

(a) to register a newspaper or publication; or

(b) to submit annual returns,

\ in accordance with this Instrument commits and offence and is liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding two hundred and fifty penalty units. F

(2) A person who after conviction under subregulation (1) continues to commit the

offence is liable on summary conviction to a fine of ten penalty units for each day the E

offence is continued. :

(3) Where there is failure to pay a fine under subregulation (1) or (2) within a period

specified by the Court, the fine is recoverable as a civil debt owed to the Republic.

## Section 9

Interpretation

In this Instrument, unless the context otherwise requires,

“Commission” means the National Media Commission; : “publication” means publication for mass communication,

“proprietor” includes the agent or representative of the proprietor and the editor. :
