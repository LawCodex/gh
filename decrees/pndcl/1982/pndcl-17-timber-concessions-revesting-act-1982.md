## SCHEDULE

P.ND.C.L.17 | TIMBER CONCESSIONS (REVESTING) ACT, 1982! AN ACT to provide for the revesting of certain timber leases and for related matters. 1. Revesting of timber concessions in the Republic The timber leases granted by the Republic the particulars of which are specified in the Schedule are hereby cancelled and the interest in the leases are hereby revested in the Republic free from all encumbrances. 2. Granting of timber leases to Mim Timber Company 7 The timber leases specified in the Schedule, the interests in which are revested in the Republic by virtue of section 1, are hereby granted to the Mim Timber Company as lessee for the remaining years of those leases subject to the same terms and conditions of those leases.”
