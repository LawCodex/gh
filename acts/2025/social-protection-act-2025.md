## Section 18

The Ministry shall collaborate with designated Ministries, Departments, Agencies and other entities to provide support to the persons specified in section 1 in the form of

(a) monetary assistance, including cash transfers; and

(6) non-monetary assistance, including in-kind support, fee waivers and subsidies to enhance access to social services. Cash transfers

## Section 19

The Ministry shall, in consultation with the Ministry responsible for Finance, provide regular cash transfers to the persons specified in section 1 through cash transfer programmes. Abuse of cash transfer

## Section 20

(1) A person shall not abuse a cash transfer programme.

(2) For the purpose of this Act, a person abuses a cash transfer programme if the person

(a) provides false information to receive a social cash grant;

(b) being a caregiver,

(i) receives cash on behalf of someone knowing that the person is no longer eligible to receive the cash transfer;

(ii) collects funds of a beneficiary who is deceased;

(iii) refuses to remit the funds due an eligible person; or

(iv) collects funds on behalf of a person who has permanently relocated outside the country; or

(c) being a service provider, an official or a representative of a financial institution or an implementing agency knowingly

(i) underpays, overpays or withholds payment to an eligible beneficiary;

(ii) denies in any other way an eligible beneficiary of the entitlement of the beneficiary without reasonable cause; or

(iii) makes payment to an ineligible person.

Termination of cash transfer

## Section 21

The appropriate authority may terminate a cash transfer to a beneficiary where

(a) the cash transfer was obtained through misrepresentation, deceit, fraud or failure to disclose information;

(6) the cash transfer was approved or paid in error;

(c) the beneficiary is no longer eligible for the cash transfer; or

(d) the beneficiary has died. Suspension of cash transfer

## Section 22

(1) The appropriate authority may suspend payment of cash transfers to, or on behalf of a person who does not access the funds for a continuous period of six months.

(2) The appropriate authority may reinstate the payment of the cash transfer where the appropriate authority is satisfied that the reasons proffered by the applicant justify a reinstatement of the cash transfer. Refund of cash transfer

## Section 23

(1) Where the appropriate authority makes payment to a person who is not entitled to the cash transfer, the amount paid in error constitutes an amount due to the State and shall be repaid by that person or, if the person is deceased, by the estate of the person.

(2) The appropriate authority shall recover the amount to which a person was not entitled.

(3) A public officer who pays a cash transfer in excess of the permitted amount shall be surcharged for the loss. Productive and financial inclusion

## Section 24

(1) The Ministry shall, in collaboration with relevant bodies, facilitate the provision of productive and financial inclusion to persons categorised as vulnerable in the second column of the First Schedule.

(d) other Ministries, Departments and Agencies and relevant non-state partners to provide support to the vulnerable persons specified in the First Schedule for access to resources and information; and

(ce) designated state agencies and non-state institutions to facilitate the provision of start-up capital to support the vulnerable persons specified in the second column of the First Schedule who have successfully completed skills acquisition programmes.

(3) The Ministry shall collaborate with the Ministry responsible for Food and Agriculture to

Emergency assistance and relief

## Section 26

(1) The Ministry shall, in collaboration with relevant agencies, identify and implement social assistance, productive inclusion and social insurance interventions in the event of emergencies.

(2) The Ministry shall collaborate with the National Disaster Management Organisation, relevant district assemblies and agencies, international partners, National Council on Persons with Disability and benevolent private contributors, where appropriate, to complement national emergency relief efforts for affected persons specified in section 1.

(3) In the event of a declaration of a national or localised emergency by the appropriate authority, the Ministry shall as far as possible, provide complementary support in the form of basic welfare needs including food aid, shelter, supply of goods and services for the affected persons specified in section 1 to ensure relief.

(4) For the purpose of subsection (3), the Ministry shall develop an eligibility criteria to identify persons to benefit from the interventions to respond to the emergency.

(5) Where appropriate, the Ministry may recommend persons benefitting from an emergency intervention to be enrolled onto other social protection interventions for a long term support.

(6) provide financial resources for the management of emergency situations; and

(6) grants, donations and other voluntary contributions; and

(c) other moneys that may become lawfully payable to the Fund. Bank account for the Fund

(4) The financial year of the Fund is the same as the financial year of the Government.

Tenure of office of members of the National Social Protection Committee

## Section 43

(1) A member of the Committee shall hold office for a period of

four years and is eligible for re-appointment for another term only.

(2) Subsection (1) does not apply to a person who is a member of the Committee by virtue of office.

(3) A member of the Committee may, at any time, resign from office in writing addressed to the Minister through the nominating authority.

(4) A member of the Committee who is absent from three consecutive meetings without sufficient cause ceases to be a member of the Committee.

(5) The Minister may, by letter addressed to a member through the nominating authority, revoke the appointment of that member.

(6) Where a member of the Committee is, for sufficient reason, unable to act as a member, the Minister shall determine whether the inability would result in the declaration of a vacancy.

(7) Where there is a vacancy

(a) under subsection (3), (4), (5) or subsection (2) of section 45, (%) as a result of a declaration under subsection (6), or

(c) by reason of the death of a member, the chairperson shall notify the Minister to appoint a person to fill the vacancy. Meetings of the National Social Protection Committee

## Section 44

(1) The Committee shall meet at least once every three months at a time and place determined by the chairperson.

(2) The chairperson shall, at the request in writing of not less than one-third of the membership of the Committee, convene an extraordinary meeting of the Committee, at a time and place determined by the chairperson.

(3) The chairperson shall preside at meetings of the Committee and in the absence of the chairperson, a member of the Committee elected by the members present from among their number shall preside.

(4) The quorum at a meeting of the Committee is seven members of the Committee.

(5) Matters before the Committee shall be decided by a majority of the members present and voting and in the event of an equality of votes, the person presiding shall have a casting vote.

GHANA MEDICAL TRUST/| The Ghana Medical Trust Fund is FUND established under the Ghana Medical Trust

Fund Act, 2025 (Act 1144) to mobilise resources to finance and support the provision of specialised medical care for the treatment of persons with chronic diseases. To achieve this object, the Fund supports approved patients in accessing specialised care, invests in programmes that promote equitable access and provides funding for specialist training and research relevant to chronic disease treatment. It also supports initiatives such as the procurement of medical equipment, development of infrastructure and human resource development. The Fund works in partnership with accredited specialist medical centres, pharmaceutical providers, laboratories and diagnostic facilities to achieve its object.
