A.F.R.C.D. 69 COMMITTEE OF INQUIRY (STATE HOUSING CORPORATION) (IMPLEMENTATION OF FINDINGS) Act, 1979?

AN ACT to provide for the forfeiture to the State of certain properties and for related matters.

## Section 1

Houses forfeited to the Republic The houses described in the first column of the First Schedule, belonging to the per-

sons specified in the second column in relation to each of those houses, are hereby for-

feited to the Republic and accordingly are hereby vested in the Republic free from all encumbrances.

## Section 2

Houses revested in the Corporation

(1) The estate houses described in the first column of the Second Schedule, belonging

to the persons specified in the second column in relation to each of the houses, are hereby

revested in the State Housing Corporation for reallocation free from all encumbrances.

(2) Despite subsection (1) a person whose estate house has been so revested may opt for a refund of the moneys paid by that person to the State Housing Corporation in respect of the house or for the moneys to be applied towards the purchase price of another estate house, but in the case of the last two named persons in the Schedule their moneys shall be refunded without any option.

## Section 3

Persons to surrender one house each to the Corporation

| (1) The persons specified in the Third Schedule shall each surrender forthwith free from all encumbrances to the State Housing Corporation one of the estate houses described in relation to that person.

(2) The State Housing Corporation shall refund the moneys paid by those persons in respect of the houses surrendered to the Corporation.

## Section 4

Corporation not liable for cost of extensions ® The State Housing Corporation is not liable to pay the cost of the extensions, improvements or renovations made to any of the houses revested in or surrendered to the Corporation under sections 2 and 3.

## Section 5

Surrender of property A person who immediately before the coming into force of this Act is in possession of any of the estate houses referred to in the First, Second and Third Schedules shall surrender it forthwith to the President or to a person authorised by the President or to the State @ Housing Corporation’.

## Section 6

Ex gratia payments The Government may, and despite anything to the contrary, make provisions for the compensation of a person who satisfies the Government regarding the granting of a loan on the security of the property forfeited to the Republic by this Act, being a loan a part of which remains for the time being unpaid.

## Section 7

Payment of moneys to the Republic Each of the persons specified in the first column of the Fourth Schedule shall pay forthwith into the Consolidated Fund the sum specified in relation to that person in the second column of the Schedule.

## Section 8

Refund to the Corporation Each of the persons specified in the first column of the Fifth Schedule shall pay forthwith to the State Housing Corporation the sum specified in relation to that person in the second column of the Schedule.

## Section 9

Offences A person who

(a) refuses or without reasonable excuse, the proof of which lies on that person, fails to surrender an estate house or pay the money required to be surrendered or paid under this Act,

(b) otherwise obstructs the President or a person authorised by the President or the State Housing Corporation in the recovery of the revesting of any of the estate houses specified in the First and Second Schedules in the Republic or

® the State Housing Corporation, commits an offence and is liable on conviction to a fine not exceeding two thousand penalty units or to a term of imprisonment not exceeding ten years or to both the fine and the imprisonment. @ 2.
