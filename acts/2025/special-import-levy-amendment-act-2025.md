# Special Import Levy Amendment Act, 2025

Act 1125

REPUBLIC OF GHANA

The One Thousand one hundred and twenty-fifth

ACT OF THE PARLIAMENT OF THE REPUBLIC OF GHANA ENTITLED

SPECIAL IMPORT LEVY (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2025 AN ACT to amend the Special Import Levy Act, 2013 (Act 861) to extend the period of application of the Special Import Levy to the end of 2028.

Date of ASSENT: 2ndApril, 2025 PASSED by Parliament and assented to by the President

Section 1 of Act 861 amended The Special Import Levy Act, 2013 (Act 861) is amended by the substitution for section 1, of “Imposition of Special Import Levy

## Section 1

There is imposed by this Act for the years 2013, 2014, 2015, 2016,2017,2018,2019,2020,2021,2022,2023,2024,2025,2026, 2027 and 2028, a Special Import Levy on imported goods to be paid at the point of entry and to be computed on the Cost, Insurance and Freight value of the goods.”. Date of Gazette notification: 2nd April, 2025.
