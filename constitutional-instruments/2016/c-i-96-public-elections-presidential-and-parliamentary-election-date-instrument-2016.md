# Public Elections (Presidential and Parliamentary Election Date) Instrument, 2016

C.I. 96

PUBLIC ELECTIONS(PRESIDENTIAL AND

PARLIAMENTARY ELECTION DATE) INSTRUMENT,2016

IN exercise of the powers conterred on the Electoral Comnussion by

article 51 of the Constitution, this Instrument is made this 20th day of July, 2016.

Date ofPresidential and Parliamentary Elections

## Section 1

The elections to the Office of the President and for members

of Parliament shaU be conducted by the Electoral Commission on Wednesday, 7th December, 2016. Voting

## Section 2

Voting shall take place throughout the country at the prescribed poUing stations on the date specified in regulation 1.

Mrs. CHARLOTTE OSEI

Chairperson, Electoral Commission

Date of Gazette notification: 22nd July, 2016.
