# Armed Forces (Amendment) (No.1) regulations, 2000

C.1. 27 ARMED FORCES (AMENDMENT) (NO.1) REGULATIONS, 2000 C.I. 12 amended The Armed Forces Regulations, 1970 (C.I. 12)' as amended are further amended by the deletion of article 6.11 (4) and the substitution therefor of the following: "6.11-Rank on enrolment-officers

(4) Candidates other than those mentioned in subparagraphs (6)-(10) of this Article, who are enrolled into the Ghana Armed Forces with University degrees or have passed the examination of any professional bodies recognised by the Ministry shall have their commission antedated two years from the effective date of the appointment, or the date of attaining 21 years of age, if later; and shall be entitled to qualification pay subject to the conditions laid down under AFR." [Date of Gazette notification: 28th January, 1998) [Entry into force: 10th May, 2000)
