# Customs, Excise (Duties and Other Taxes) (Amendment) Act, 2008 (Act 751)

ACT 751

CUSTOMS, EXCISE (DUTIES AND OTHER TAXES) (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2008 (ACT 751) THE SEVEN HUNDRED AND FIFTY-FIRST ACT OF THE PARLIAMENT OF THE REPUBLIC OF GHANA ENTITLED CUSTOMS, EXCISE (DUTIES AND OTHER TAXES) (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2008 An Act to amend the Customs, Excise (Duties and Other Taxes), Act 1996, Act 512 as amended to abolish import duty on the importation of telephone sets including mobile, cellular and satellite phones. DATE OF ASSENT: 28th March, 2008. ENACTED by the President and Parliament: First Schedule of Act 512 as amended further amended The Customs, Excise (Duties and Other Taxes), Act 1996, Act 512 is further amended as follows: The import duty on telephone sets including mobile, cellular and satellite phones specified in the First Schedule is abolished. Date of Gazette Notification: 28th March, 2008.
