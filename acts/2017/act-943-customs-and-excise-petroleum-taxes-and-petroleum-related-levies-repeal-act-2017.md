# Customs and Excise (Petroleum Taxes and Petroleum Related Levies) (Repeal) Act, 2017 (Act 943)

ACT 943

ACUSTOMS AND EXCISE (PETROLEUM TAXES AND PETROLEUM RELATED LEVIES) (REPEAL) ACT, 2017 (ACT 943) ARRANGEMENT OF SECTIONS Section

## Section 1

Act 685 repealed

## Section 2

Transitional provision THE NINE HUNDRED AND FORTY-THIRD ACT OF THE PARLIAMENT OF THE REPUBLIC OF GHANA ENTITLED CUSTOMS AND EXCISE (PETROLEUM TAXES AND PETROLEUM RELATED LEVIES) (REPEAL) ACT, 2017 AN ACT to repeal the Customs and Excise (Petroleum Taxes and Petroleum Related Levies) Act, 2005 (Act 685). DATE OF ASSENT: 15th March, 2017. PASSED by Parliament and assented to by the President:

## Section 1 — Act 685 repealed

The Customs and Excise (Petroleum Taxes And Petroleum Related Levies) Act, 2005 (Act 685) is repealed.

## Section 2 — Transitional provision

Any rights, liabilities and obligations in existence immediately before the repeal of this Act shall continue to exist until concluded or resolved. Date of Gazette Notification: 15th March, 2017.
