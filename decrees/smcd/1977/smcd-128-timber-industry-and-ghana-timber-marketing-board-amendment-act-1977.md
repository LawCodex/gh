Offences

(1) A person who contravenes a provision of section | or 2 commits an offence and is > liable on summary conviction to a term of imprisonment not less than fifteen years and ; not more than thirty years.

(2) The Court convicting the accused may, if satisfied on grounds stated that the offence was trivial or that there are special circumstances relating to the offence or the offender which would render unjust the application of the minimum penalty prescribed by subsection (1), impose a term of imprisonment less than the minimum or a fine not exceeding three thousand penalty units or both the less term and the fine.

(3) Unless the Court otherwise decides on grounds stated, the goods involved in the ‘y commission of an offence under this section shall be forfeited to the Republic.

## Section 4

Fixing of local prices

(1) Despite an enactment to the contrary the Board shall, to the exclusion of any other person, have the right to fix the prices at which the timber products specified in the Schedule and any other timber products prescribed by the Minister by Regulations made under section 12, shall be exported.

(2) The Board shall advise the Prices and Incomes Board and the Minister as to the price at which the timber products shall be sold within Ghana.

## Section 5

Demand of returns

The Board may by writing request a manufacturer of any timber products to submit to the Board returns of the products manufactured by that manufacturer and of sales made of those products.

## Section 6

Percentage for export of timber products

The Board may also give directions to a manufacturer of timber products relating to the percentage of the products manufactured by that manufacturer which should be exported and the percentage to be sold in Ghana.

## Section 7

Registration of dealers

A person shall not, after three months from the commencement of this Act, operate as a retailer, wholesaler or dealer of or in plywood, timber, flush doors and any other timber products prescribed by the Minister by the Regulations unless that person is registered og with the Board and has a valid licence from the Board so to do, 4
