# Oti Region Instrument, 2019 C.I.112

C.I. 112

OTI REGION INSTRUMENT, 2019

WHEREAS, the President may, by constitutional instrument, create a new region pursuant to paragraph (a) of clause (1) of article 5 of the Constitution;

WHEREAS,in 2017,the President received petitions from many citizens and traditional rulers from several regions across the country, namely

Brong-Ahafo, Northern, Volta and Western Regions, demanding the creation of new regions;

WHEREAS, by letter dated 26"* June, 2017, the President, acting in accordance with clause (2) of article 5 of the Constitution, referred the

petitions to the Council of State for its advice; WHEREAS,the Council of State reviewed the petitions and concluded

that there was a need and substanticd demand for the creation of regions,

and, accordingly advised the President, by letter dated 15"* August, 2017, to appoint a commission ofinquiry to inquire into the need and demand for the creation of regions;

WHEREAS, the President, pursuant to clause (2) of article 5 of the Constitution, acting in accordance with the advice of the Council of State, by constitutional instrument, the Commission of Inquiry into the Creation of New Regions Instrument, 2017(C.I.105), appointed a Commission of Inquiry into the Creation of New Regions (the "Commission"),to inquire into the demands and make recommendations on all factors involved in the creation of regions;

WHEREAS, the Commission conducted its inquiry tmd established in

its report, dated 26"* June, 2018 and titled, "Equitable Distribution of National Resources for Balanced Development", that there was a substantial demand and need in the areas from which the petitions emanated;

C.l. 112 2 Ott Region Instrument, 2019 WHEREAS, the Commission on that basis, recommended

(a) the creation of new regions out of Brong-Ahafo, Northern, Volta and Western Regions; .

(b) that referenda be held only in the areas within the boundaries of the proposed regions; and ;

(c) that only registered voters within the proposed regions should be eligible to participate in the referenda; WHEREAS, on 26 June, 2018, the Commission presented its report and recommendations to the President; WHEREAS, the President, upon receipt of the report and recommendations of the Commission, and in accordance with clause (5) of article 5 of the Constitution, forwarded, by letter dated 18" July, 2018, the recommendations of the Commission to the Electoral Commission, to hold referenda in the manner prescribed in the report of the Commission; WHEREAS, on 27" December, 2018, a referendum was held, under the supervision of the Electoral Commission, in the proposed Oti Region, pursuant to which at least fifty per cent of the registered voters within the proposed Oti Region voted overwhelmingly in favour of the creation of the new region, meeting the constitutional threshold of at least eighty per cent of the votes cast, being in favour of creating the new region, 45 set out in Gazette Number 12 of 2019; WHEREAS, by clause (8) of article 5 of the Constitution, the President is required to issue a constitutional instrument giving effect, or enabling effect to be given, to the results of the referendum; NOW, THEREFORE, in exercise of the power conferred on the President by clauses (1) and (8) of article 5 of the Constitution, this Instrument is made this Ist day of February, 2019.

3 C.l. 112 Oti Region Instrument, 2019 Creation of Region The Oti Region is created out of the Volta Region.
