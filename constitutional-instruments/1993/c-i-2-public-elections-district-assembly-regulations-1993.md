Local government electoral area The electoral areas specified in the third column of the First Schedule to the Instrument establishing a District Assembly shall be the local government electoral area for the purposes of District Assembly elections. 2. One member for each electoral area Each electoral area shall be represented by one member in a District Assembly. | 3. Agents of candidate |

(1) For the purposes of a local government election, a candidate may appoint one agent to attend at the polling station to witness the poll and the counting of votes.

(2) A candidate may appoint not more than two agents to attend at the electoral area centre for purposes of collating the votes from the polling stations. ff >, , 1. These Regulations have been superseded by the Public Elections (District Assembly) Regulations, 1993 } (CL. 4). 1-287 [Issue 1]
