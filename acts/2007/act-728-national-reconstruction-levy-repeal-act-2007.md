ACT 728

THE SEVEN HUNDRED AND TWENTY-EIGHTH ACT OF THE PARLIAMENT OF THE REPUBULIC OF GHANA ENTITLED NATIONAL RECONSTRUCTION LEVY (REPEAL) ACT, 2007 AN ACT to repeal the National Reconstruction Levy Act, 2001 (Act 597). DATE OF ASSENT: 5th April, 2007. ENACTED by the President and Parliament as follows:

## Section 1 — Act 597 repealed

The National Reconstruction Levy Act, 2001 (Act 597) is repealed.

## Section 2 — Commencement

This Act shall come into force on 1st January 2007. Date of Gazette Notification: 13th April, 2007.
