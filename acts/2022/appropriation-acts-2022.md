ACT OF THE PARLIAMENT OF THE REPUBLIC OF GHANA ENTITLED APPROPRIATION ACT, 2022 AN ACT to provide for the withdrawal of sums of money necessary to meet Government expenditure for the 2023 financial year from the Consolidated Fund and other public funds and for related matters. DATE OF ASSENT: 29'h December, 2022. PASSED by Parliament and assented to by the President Sum of money to be issued from the Consolidated Fund for 2023 fmancial year

## Section 1

Pursuant to paragraph (a) of clause (2) of article 179 of the Constitution, a sum of money not exceeding Two Hundred and Twenty-Seven Billion, Seven Hundred and TwentyOne Million, One Hundred and Eighty-Two Thousand, Nine Hundred and Fifty-Eight Ghana Cedis (GH¢227,721,182,958) shall be issued from the Consolidated Fund and other public funds during the financial year commencing on the I" day of January, 2023 and ending on the 31 '' day of December, 2023 and shall be appropriated for the purposes specified in the First Schedule, which translates into the Third and Fourth Schedules representing Ministries, Departments and Agencies and Other Government Obligations with their programmes. Retained Internally Generated Funds

## Section 2

The Ministries, Departments, and Agencies listed in the first column of the Second Schedule may retain and use an amount of Ten Billion, Six Hundred and Twenty-Seven Million, Six Hundred and Fifteen Thousand, Two Hundred and Sixty-Eight Ghana Cedis (GH¢10,627,615,268) indicated in the second column of the Second Schedule being a part of the total amount specified in section I during the fmancial year commencing on the I'' day of January, 2023 and ending on the 31'' day of December, 2023. Payment of money on authorisation of Minister responsible for Finance

## Section 3

The Controller and Accountant-General may, on a warrant issued on the authority of the Minister responsible for Finance, pay out of the Consolidated Fund and other public funds during the financial year ending on the 31" day of December, 2023 any sum not exceeding the

sum of Two Hundred and Twenty-Seven Billion, Seven Hundred and Twenty-One Million, One Hundred and Eighty-Two Thousand, Nine Hundred and Fifty-Eight Ghana Cedis (GH¢227,721,182,958) referred to in section 1. Repeal

## Section 4

The Appropriation (No.2) Act, 2021 (Act 1074) is repealed. Commencement

## Section 5

This Act shall come into force on the 1'' day of January, 2023.
