## Section 1

Declaration of selected area

(1) Where it appears to the President in the public interest so to do, the President may by executive instrument declare an area of state or stool land to be a selected area, and, accordingly, on the making of the instrument, that area shall for the purposes of this Act be a selected area and titles to land therein shall be granted as prescribed under this Act.

(2) Subject to article 267 of the Constitution, where a stool land in respect of which | an executive instrument is made under subsection (1) of this section, has not been vested in the President under section 7 of the Administration of Lands Act, 1962 (Act 123), the instrument in respect of that land shall have the same effect as an instrument made under the section 7.

## Section 3

Plan and Schedule to plan

(1) There shali be made a plan as prescribed of a selected area and there shall be specified in the prescribed manner on the plan a schedule setting out in entries the lands granted under section 2 and in corresponding entries the names of the grantees, the interests held by them in the land, the dates from which the grants take effect and any other particulars as may be prescribed.

(2) The Minister shall make an endorsement below the last entry that the particulars specified in the plan and the schedule to the plan are in accordance with the provisions of this Act and any other enactment in that behalf and are true and accurate and shail sign and date that endorsement.
