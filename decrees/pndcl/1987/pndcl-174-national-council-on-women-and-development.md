Interim management committee

(1) Until provision is otherwise made by law, the functions of the National Council on Women and Development under the National Council on Women and Development Decree, 1975 (N.R.C.D. 322) shall be exercised by an interim management committee established under subsection (2) of this section.

(2) The interim management committee shall consist of the chairman and nine other persons all of whom shall be appointed by the President.
