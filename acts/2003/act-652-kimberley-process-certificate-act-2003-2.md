## Section 1

Kimberley Process Certificate required for export of rough diamonds A person who has been granted a licence under section 3 of the Mineral and Mining Act, 19867 shall not export rough diamonds unless that person has applied for and been i issued a Kimberley Process Certificate. :

## Section 2

Application for Kimberley Process Certificate An application for a Kimberley Process Certificate shall be as in the Form 1 set out in the Schedule and shall be made to the Minister.

## Section 3

Issue and form of Kimberley Process Certificate } (1) The Minister, may when all the information required to be given under section 2 is supplied, issue a Kimberley Process Certificate in respect of the rough diamonds to which the application relates, within three days from the date of submission of the application.

(2) An applicant who does not comply with section 2 shall not be issued with a Kimberley Process Certificate. 1. The Act was assented to on 24th October, 2003. a 2. P.N.D.C.L, 153.

(3) The Kimberley Process Certificate shall contain the information provided in the Form 2 set out in the Schedule. 4, Export report —_

(1) An exporter of rough diamonds shall prepare a report which shall be as in the Form 3 set out in the Schedule.

(2) Form 3 shall be presented to a customs officer at the time of export and a copy shall be sent to the Minister by mail, courier or delivered by hand within seven days after the export.

## Section 5

Import report .

(1) An importer of rough diamonds shall prepare a report which shall be as in the Form 4 set out in the Schedule and shall be presented to a customs officer at the time of clearing the import.

(2) Form 4 shall be accompanied with a Kimberley Process Certificate issued by the exporting authority responsible for rough diamonds in the country of origin of the rough diamonds.

(3) A copy of the Form 4 and the Kimberley Process Certificate from the exporter shail be sent to the Minister by mail, courier or delivered by hand within seven days after the importation of the rough diamonds.

## Section 6

Packaging of rough diamonds for export and import

(1) Rough diamonds shall be exported or imported in containers in accordance with this Act.

(2) A container for the export and import of rough diamonds shall be constructed in a manner that makes the reseal of the container impossible after it is opened.

(3) The container in which rough diamonds are exported shall bear the serial number of the accompanying Kimberley Process Certificate.

## Section 7

Record keeping of rough diamonds for export and import A person who exports or imports rough diamonds shall keep in respect of the rough diamonds the following records:

(a) the serial numbers of the Kimberley Process Certificate, and

(b) the purchase order, invoice or similar document if the rough diamonds , were purchased or otherwise obtained by the exporter or importer. :

## Section 8

Suspension and cancellation of licence

(1) The Minister may suspend or cancel a licence issued to an exporter of rough diamonds under section | if the holder

(a) fails to submit a report required under section 4 or 5,

## Section 9

Offence

(1) A person who is in breach of a provision of this Act commits an offence and is liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding five hundred penalty units or a term of imprisonment not exceeding two years or to both the fine and imprisonment.

(2) The diamonds, the subject of the conviction shall be forfeited to the State.
