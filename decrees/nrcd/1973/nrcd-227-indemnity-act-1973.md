## Section 2

Indemnity for acts done under Decrees A Court shall not entertain an action in any civil proceedings against the Republic, the National Redemption Council, the Executive Council, a member of the Armed Forces or of the Police Service, or any other person, in respect of an act or omission done or purported to be done in good faith in pursuance of an Act of the National Redemption Council or any other enactment.

## Section 2A

Indemnity for acts done between 13th January, 1972 and 13th January, 1973 Notwithstanding any other provision of this Act, an action or proceedings shali not be brought or proceeded with, against the Republic, the National Redemption Council, the Executive Council, a member of the Armed Forces or of the Police Force in respect of an act or omission which is alleged to have been done or purported to have been done by a member of the Armed Forces or of the Police Force during the period commencing on the 13th of January, 1972 and ending with the 13th of January, 1973.7
