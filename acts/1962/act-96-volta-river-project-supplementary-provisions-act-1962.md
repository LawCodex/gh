AN ACT to exempt certain agreements relating to the Volta River Project from the provisions of sections 5 and 6 of the Contracts Act, 1960 (Act 25) and to provide : for certain financial matters connected with the Project. 1. ## Section 4

Interpretation

(1) In this Act, the “Master Agreement” means the Master Agreement entered into between the Government of Ghana and the Volta Aluminium Company Limited in the terms approved by the National Assembly in January, 1962.
