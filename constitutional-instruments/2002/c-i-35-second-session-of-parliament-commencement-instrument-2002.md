# Second Session of Parliament (Commencement) Instrument, 2002 C.I 35

C.I. 35 SECOND SESSION OF PARLIAMENT (COMMENCEMENT) INSTRUMENT, 2002 KNOW YE ALL MEN that in the exercise of the powers conferred on the Speaker of Parliament by Article 112 of the Constitution, I, PETER ALA ADJETEY, Speaker of Parliament, do by this Constitutional Instrument appoint Parliament House, Accra, to be the place and Ten (10) o'clock in the forenoon of Tuesday, the 15th day of January, 2002, to be the time at which the First Sitting of the Second Session of the Third Parliament of the Fourth Republic shall commence. Given under my hand this 4th day of January, 2002. PETER ALA ADJETEY Speaker Date of Gazette Notification: 4th January, 2002.
