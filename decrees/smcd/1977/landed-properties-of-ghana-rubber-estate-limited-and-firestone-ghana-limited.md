The term of lease and freehold interest granted by Government to Ghana Rubber Estates and Firestone Ghana Limited respectively shall remain valid

(1) Despite a law, decree or regulation to the contrary, the term of ninety-nine years granted, under the Lease dated the 3rd day of June, 1968, by the Government of Ghana to Ghana Rubber Estates Limited shall remain in full force and effect.

(2) Despite a law, decree or regulations to the contrary, the freehold interest granted under the Conveyance dated the 3rd of June, 1978 by the Government of Ghana to Firestone Ghana Limited shall remain in full force and effect.
