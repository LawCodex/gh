# Energy Sector Levies (Amendment) Act, 2017 (Act 946)

ACT 946

ENERGY SECTOR LEVIES (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2017 (ACT 946) ARRANGEMENT OF SECTIONS Section

## Section 1

Section 4 of Act 899 amended

## Section 2

First Schedule to Act 899 amended THE NINE HUNDRED AND FORTY-SIXTH ACT OF THE PARLIAMENT OF THE REPUBLIC OF GHANA ENTITLED ENERGY SECTOR LEVIES (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2017 AN ACT to amend the Energy Sector Levies Act, 2015 (Act 899) to review tranfers into the Power Generation and Infrasructure Support Sub-Account; to provide for a reduction in the rate of levies for Public Lighting and the National Electrification Scheme on petroleum products and for related matters. DATE OF ASSENT: 5th April, 2017. PASSED by Parliament and assented to by the President:

## Section 1 — Section 4 of Act 899 amended

The Energy Sector Levies Act, 2015 (Act 899) referred to in this enactment as the "principal enactment" is amended in subsection (2) of section 4 by the repeal of paragraphs (a) and (b).
