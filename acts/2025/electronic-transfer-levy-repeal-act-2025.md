REPUBLIC OF GHANA

The One thousand one hundred and twenty-seventh

ACT OF THE PARLIAMENT OF THE REPUBLIC OF GHANA ENTITLED ELECTRONIC TRANSFER LEVY (REPEAL) ACT, 2025

AN ACT to repeal the Electronic Transfer Levy Act, 2022 (Act 1075) and the Electronic Transfer Levy (Amendment) Act, 2022 (Act 1089) and to provide for related matters.

Date of Assent: 2ndApril, 2025 PASSED by Parliament and assented to by the President Act 1075 and Act 1089 repealed 1. The following enactments are repealed:

(a) the Electronic Transfer Levy Act, 2022 (Act 1075); and

(b) the Electronic Transfer Levy (Amendment) Act, 2022 (Act 1089).

Savings 2. Despite the repeal of the enactments specified under section 1, a right, a liability or an obligation in existence immediately before the repeal of those enactments shall continue in existence until the right, liability or obligation is exercised or terminated.
