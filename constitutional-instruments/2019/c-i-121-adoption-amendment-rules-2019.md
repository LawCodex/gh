IN exercise of the power conferred on the Rules of Court Committee by clause (2) of article 157 of the Constitution, these Rules are made this 9th day of October, 2019.

## Section 1

Rule 1 of C.I. 42 amended The Adoption Rules, 2003 (C.I. 42) referred to in these Rules as the “principal enactment” is amended by the substitution for rule 1, of

“1, Application of Rules

These Rules apply to adoption proceedings.”.

## Section 2

Rules 1A and 1B inserted The principal enactment is amended by the insertion after rule 1, of

“1A, Jurisdiction for adoption proceedings ‘

The High Court shall have exclusive original jurisdiction in matters relating to adoption. 1B. Application for adoption order A person who seeks to obtain an adoption order sh 1 to the Court as in Form 1 set out in Schedule 1.”, t shall apply

## Section 3

Rule 2 of C.I. 42 amended The principal enactment is amended by the substitution for rule 2. of “2. Adoption proceedings and Social Enquiry Report h nie, Proceedings on adoption shall be conducted in chambers in the Court under the High Court Civil Pr 3004 (CL. 47). ocedure Rules,

(2) No person other than

(a) the applicant,

(b) the lawyer of the applicant,

(c) the child who is the subject of the application for an adoption order,

(d) a probation officer or social worker of the Department representing the interest of the child, and

(e) officers of the Court, shall be present at the hearing of an application for an adoption order unless the presiding judge determines that there are exceptional circumstances that require otherwise.

(3) Where the applicant is a relative of the child, the parents, guardian or the personal representatives of the parents of the child shall be present at the hearing of an application for an adoption order.

(4) Where consent is required for the grant of an adoption order, the Court shall require documentary evidence of the consent. .

(5) The Court shall, as a condition for the grant ofan adoption order, require a probation officer or a social worker of the Department, to prepare a Social Enquiry Report to assist the Court to determine whether the adoption is in the best interest of the child.”.

## Section 4

Rule 4 of C.I. 42 amended The principal enactment is amended by the substitution for rule 4, of “4, Director to be guardian ad litem The Director shall be the guardian ad litem of the child and a copy of the application shall be served on the Department which shall then enter appearance.”. 5, Rule 23 of C.I. 42 amended The principal enactment is amended in rule 23 by the insertion after “Act,” of ““child’ means the child who is the subject of the application for an adoption order; and “Court” means the High Court within the jurisdiction where the child resides at the time of the application;”.
