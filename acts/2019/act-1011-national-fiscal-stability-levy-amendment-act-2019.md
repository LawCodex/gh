# National Fiscal Stability Levy (Amendment) Act, 2019 (Act 1011)

NATIONAL FISCAL STABILISATION LEVY (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2019 (ACT 1011) ARRANGEMENT OF SECTIONS Section

## Section 1

Section 4 of Act 862 amended

## Section 2

Repeal ACT 1011

THE ONE THOUSAND AND ELEVENTH ACT OF THE PARLIAMENT OF THE REPUBLIC OF GHANA ENTITLED NATIONAL FISCAL STABILISATION LEVY (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2019 AN ACT to amend the National Fiscal Stabilisation Levy Act, 2013 (Act 862) to extend the date of the expiration of the National Fiscal Stabilisation Levy and for related matters. DATE OF ASSENT: 28th December. 2019. PASSED by Parliament and assented to by the President

## Section 1 — Section 4 of Act 862 amended

The National Fiscal Stabilisation Levy Act, 2013 (Act 862) is amended by the substitution for section 4 of— "Profits affected by Levy

## Section 4

The Levy is payable in respect of profits before tax for the 2013, 2014, 2015, 2016, 2017, 2018, 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023 and 2024 years of assessment.".

## Section 2 — Repeal

The National Fiscal Stabilisation Levy (Amendment) Act, 2014 (Act 882) is repealed. Date of Gazette Notification: 30th December, 2019.
