Consequential amendments. sn, 4. Commencement. POLICE SERVICE (PENSIONS) ACT, 1985' AN ACT to provide separately for pensions for members of the Police Service and for related matters. 1. Disapplication of S.M.C.D. 8 to Police Service On the coming into force of this Act the provisions of the Pensions and Social Security (Amendment) Decree, 1975 (S.M.C.D. 8) shall cease to be applicable to the members of the Police Service, and a member of the Police Service who before the commencement of this Act is required by virtue of the Pensions and Social Security (Amendment) Decree, 1975 (S.M.C.D. 8) to contribute towards the pension or to the social security fund as a member of that fund shall, on the coming into force of this Act, cease to make that contribution towards the pension or to the social security fund. 2. Refund of accumulated contributions

(1) Subject to subsection (2), the amounts paid by or on behalf of a member of the Police Service as contributions towards that member’s pension or social security fund before the commencement of this Act shall be refunded to that member,

(a) when that member retires from the Police Service on attaining the retire- : ment age prescribed under the Police Service Act, 1970 (Act 350); or

(2) The amount payable to a member of the Police Service under subsection (1) shall be paid to the estate if that member dies before that member is due to retire from the Police Service or the retiring awards are paid to that member or before attaining the age of superannuation. lt.
