# Fourth Session of Parliament (Commencement) Instrument, 2000 C.I 26

C.I. 26 FOURTH SESSION OF PARLIAMENT (COMMENCEMENT) INSTRUMENT, 2000 KNOW YE ALL MEN that in exercise of the powers conferred on the Speaker by clause (1) of article 112 of the Constitution, I DANIEL FRANCIS ANNAN, Speaker of Parliament, by this Constitutional Instrument, appoint Parliament House to be the place and ten o'clock in the morning of Tuesday, 11th January 2000, to be the time at which the Fourth Session of the Second Parliament of the Fourth Republic of Ghana shall commence. Given under my hand this 6th day of January, 2000. JUSTICE DANIEL FRANCIS ANNAN Speaker Date of Gazette Notification: 7th January, 2000.
