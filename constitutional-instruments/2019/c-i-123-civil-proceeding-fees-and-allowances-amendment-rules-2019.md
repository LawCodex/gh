I. 123 2 CIVIL PROCEEDINGS (FEES AND ALLOWANCES) (AMENDMENT) RULES, 2019 IN exercise of the power conferred on the Rules of Court Committee by clause (2) of article 157 of the Constitution, these Rules are made this 9th day of October, 2019. First Schedule to C.I. 55 amended 1. Alternative Dispute Resolution Fee 10.00”. Second Schedule to C.I. 55 amended 2. The principal enactment is amended in the Second Schedule by the insertion after paragraph 3, of “4. Service of Pre-Trial Processes 30.00”.
