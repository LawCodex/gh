UNCLAIMED PROPERTY ACT, 1969' |

AN ACT to provide for the disposal of unclaimed property and for related matters.

## Section 1

Unclaimed property

Where an article has been left or found in or on a Government premises, and the /

owner is unknown or cannot reasonably be found, the article shall be disposed of as

provided by this Act.

## Section 2

Arms, jewels, precious metals |

Where an article is a firearm, arm, ammunition, explosive, gunpowder, munitions of

war or offensive weapon of any description or an article similar to any of them, or con-

sists of precious stones or precious metal, a senior officer of the premises in or on which |

it has been left or found shall hand it over to the officer in charge of the nearest police

station.

‘ 3. Perishable and unsaleable goods | Where an article is of a perishable nature or in a condition which is wholly unsaleable

it may be destroyed at any time on the written authority of a senior officer of the premises

in or on which it has been left or found.

## Section 5

Procedure in other cases

(1) In all other cases, a senior officer of the premises in or on which an article has been left or found shall post a notice conspicuously outside the building which the senior officer administers specifying the article left or found on the premises or land belonging to the premises.

(2) Where the article remains unclaimed after the notice has been posted for one ; month it shall be sold by public auction. ON

(3) Where an auctioneer is not readily available, the senior officer or a person appointed by the senior officer for the purpose may lawfully hold the auction, despite a law to the contrary.

(4) After deducting the expenses of sale, the senior officer shall pay the balance to the Controller and the Accountant-General.

## Section 6

Repayment to owner Where a person who was or appears to the Controller and Accountant-General to have been the owner of an article previous to the sale applies to the Controller and Accountant- General within one year after the sale, the Controller and Accountant-General shall pay the sum received to that person.

## Section 7

Record to be kept The senior officer of a Government premises in or on which an article has been left or found shall keep a written record of the article, specifying the manner in which it was disposed of, and if sold the amount realised by the sale and the expenses incurred.

## Section 8

Application This Act shall not apply to property in the possession of the police.

## Section 9

Repeals The Derelict Property Ordinance (Cap. 261) is hereby repealed. {Issue 1] VIE — 3352
