Local Government electoral area The electoral areas specified in the third column of the First Schedule to the Instrument establishing a District Assembly shall be the local government electoral area for the purposes of District Assembly elections. : | 2. (1) For the purposes of a local government election a candidate may appoint one : agent to attend at the polling station to witness the poll and the counting of votes.
