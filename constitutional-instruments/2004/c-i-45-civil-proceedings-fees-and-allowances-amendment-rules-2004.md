## SCHEDULE

Schedule IN exercise of the powers conferred on the Electoral Commission by article 47 clauses (1) and (5) and article 51 of the Constitution, this Instrument is made this 26th day of January, 2004.

### Section 1 — Constituencies for Election to Parliament

(1) Ghana is divided into two hundred and thirty Constituencies for the purposes of election of members of the Parliament which will hold its first sitting in January, 2005, and for subsequent elections.

(2) Each Constituency shall be represented by one member in Parliament.

### Section 2 — Area of Constituencies

The electoral areas specified in the third column of the Schedule to this Instrument are hereby determined as the areas of the constituencies specified in the first column of the Schedule and the constituency headquarters are as specified in the second column of the Schedule.

### Section 3 — Revocation and Transitional Provision

(1) The Representation of the People (Parliamentary Constituencies) Instrument, 1992 (L.I. 1538) is hereby revoked.

(2) Notwithstanding the revocation under subregulation (1), a person representing a constituency under the revoked instrument shall continue to hold office until the Parliament is dissolved.
