N.R.C.D. 299 YENDI SKIN AFFAIRS ACT, 1974 WHEREAS on the 18th day of August, 1972 the National Redemption Council appointed | a Committee of Inquiry to inquire into certain matters relating to the Yendi Skin: AND WHEREAS the said Committee found that the purported nomination, selection and enskinment of Mahamadu Abudulai as Ya-Na was null and void: AND WHEREAS the National Redemption Council deems it fit to give due effect to the said | finding of the said Committee of Inquiry: NOW WHEREAS, in pursuance of the National Redemption Council (Establishment) Proclamation, 1972 this Act is hereby made. 1. Mahamadu Abudulai’s enskinment, etc. declared null and void The purported nomination, selection and enskinment of Mahamadu Abudulai as Ya- Na and Paramount Chief of Dagbon is hereby declared to be null and void for all purposes, whether under customary law or otherwise, 2. Mahamadu Abudulai not to purport to be chief \ (1) Mahamadu Abudulai shall not : (a) purport to be Ya-Na or Paramount Chief of Dagbon whether under customary law or otherwise;

(b) in any manner assert any claim to be Ya-Na or Paramount Chief of Dagbon; ‘

(c) do any act tending to incite disaffection against Ya-Na Yakubu IL, Paramount Chief of Dagbon; or Vil — 4601 [issue IJ

(2) If Mahamadu Abudulai contravenes any provision of subsection (1) of this section Mahamadu Abudulai commits an offence and is liable on conviction to a term of imprisonment not exceeding ten years without the option of a fine. :

## Section 3

No person to recognise Mahamadu Abudulai

(1) A person shall not

(a) recognise Mahamadu Abudulai as Ya-Na or Paramount Chief of Dagbon whether under customary law or otherwise;

(b) in any manner support, encourage or canvass any claim of Mahamadu Abudulai to be Ya-Na or Paramount Chief of Dagbon, _

(c) do any act tending to incite disaffection against Ya-Na Yakubu JU, Paramount Chief of Dagbon.

(2) Any person who contravenes any provision of subsection (1) commits an offence and is liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding one penalty unit or to imprisonment not exceeding ten years or to both. | 4, Application The provision of this Act shall apply despite an enactment, a customary law or any other law to the contrary. {Issue 1] VII — 4602
