# Public Elections (Presidential and Parliamentary Election Date) Regulations, 2004 C.I. 48

C.1. 48 PUBLIC ELECTIONS (PRESIDENTIAL AND PARLIAMENTARY ELECTION DATE) INSTRUMENT, 2004

## Section 1

Date of Presidential and Parliamentary Elections

The elections to the Office of President and for membership of Parliament shall be conducted by the Electoral Commission on Tuesday, 7th December, 2004.

## Section 2

Voting

Voting shall take place throughout the country at the prescribed polling stations on the date specified in this Instrument.
