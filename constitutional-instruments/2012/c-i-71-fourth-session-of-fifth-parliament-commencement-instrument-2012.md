C. I. 71 FOURTH

SESSION OF FIFTH PARLIAMENT

(COMMENCEMENT) INSTRUMENT, 2012 KNOW YE ALL MEN ■ that in exercise of the powers conferred on the Speaker by clause (1) of article 112 of the Constitution, I, Rt. Hon.

Justice Joyce Adeline Bamford-Addo, Speaker of Parliament of Ghana, by this Constitutional Instrument, appoint Parliament House, Accra, to be the place, and ten o'clock in the forenoon of Tuesday, 24th January, 2012 to be the time at which the Fourth Session of the Fifth Parliament of the Fourth Republic of Ghana shall commence.

Given under my hand this 5th January, 2012.

RT. HON. JUSTICE JOYCE ADELINE BAMFORD-ADDO Speaker ofFarliament

Date of

notification: 6th January,2012.
