P.N.D.C.L. 135 STATISTICAL SERVICE ACT, 1985' sy AN ACT pursuant to clause (1) of article 190 of the Constitution to provide for nd matters relating to the Statistical Service. Establishment

## Section 1

Membership of Service The Statistical Service established pursuant to clause (1) of article 185 and clause (1) of article 190 of the Constitution is made up of

(a) the personnel of the Central Bureau of Statistics established under the Statistics Act, 1961 (Act 37),

(b) any other public officers that may be transferred or seconded to the Service, and

(c) any other persons that may be employed for the Service.

## Section 2

The Board

(1) The governing body of the Service is the Statistical Service Board consisting of

(a) the chairman and not more than five other members, and

(b) the Government Statistician as an ex-officio member of the Board.

(2) The members of the Board shall be appointed by the President in accordance with article 186 of the Constitution,

## Section 3

Functions of the Board The Board is responsible for

(a) promoting the effective use of statistics and stimulating research activities - in the fields of application of statistics; °

(b) ensuring that the Service collects and compiles socio-economic data on Ghana and presents them to relevant government agencies requiring that information;

(c) advising the Government on the streamlining of departments of Government responsible for data collection; 1. The Act was issued as the Statistical Service Law, 1985 (P.N.D.C.L. 135) made on 3rd December, 1985 and notified in the Gazette on 24th December, 1985. \ {Issue 1] VII — 1852

(d) determining the manner in which data may be compiled by a person or authority in Ghana; and ; (e) determining which data may be published and the manner in which the data is published.

## Section 4

Tenure of office of members of Board

(1) A member of the Board, other than the Government Statistician, shall hold office | for a period not exceeding three years, and is eligible for re-appointment.

(2) A member of the Board may resign from office as a member in writing addressed co to the President and may be removed from office by the President. td (3) Except as otherwise provided members of the Board hold office on the terms and conditions, including the payment of allowances or remuneration determined by the President. |

## Section 5

Meetings of the Board

(1) The Board shall meet at least once in every three months, at the times and places determined by the chairman.

(2) A special meeting of the Board shall be called on a request signed by the Government Statistician addressed to the chairman of the Board. |

(3) The chairman shall preside at the meetings of the Board and in the absence of the chairman, a member of the Board appointed by the members present from among themselves shall preside.

(4) The quorum of every meeting of the Board is four.

(5) The Board may co-opt a person to act as an adviser at its meeting but a person so co-opted is not entitled to vote at a meeting on a matter for decision by the Board.

(6) The validity of the proceedings of the Board shall not be affected by a vacancy in | its membership or by a defect in the appointment of a member of the Board.

(7) Subject to the provisions of this Act, the Board shall regulate its own procedure.

## Section 6

Committees of the Board | The Board may for the performance of its functions appoint committees and may assign | to a committee the functions that the Board may determine. ; Government Statistician |

## Section 7

The Government Statistician | The head of the Service is the Government Statistician who shall be appointed by the President in accordance with article 185 of the Constitution.

## Section 8

Duties of Government Statistician \ (1) The functions of the Government Statistician are VIE — 1853 [Issue 1]

(a) to advise the Government and the Board on matters relating to statistics;

(b) to collect, compile, analyse, abstract and publish statistical information S relating to the commercial, industrial, agricultural, social, financial, ecoi nomic and any other activities and conditions of residents of Ghana;

(c) to conduct statistical surveys, including a census in Ghana;

(d) to organise a co-ordinated scheme of economic and social statistics relating to Ghana; and

(e) to publish the socio-economic data on Ghana that the Board may permit.

(2) Subject to the supervision of the Board, the Government Statistician is responsible . for the day-to-day administration of the Service. PY

## Section 9

Collaboration with organisations

Public Services and other official or quasi-official organisations or any other organisation shall collaborate with the Government Statistician in the collection, compilation, analysis and publication of statistical records of or connected with the Public Services or those organisations.

## Section 10

Deputy of Government Statistician

(1) There shall be two Deputy Government Statisticians who shall be appointed by the President in accordance with article 195 of the Constitution.

(2) The two Deputy Government Statisticians shall assist the Government Statistician in the performance of functions and shall perform any other functions that the Government Statistician may in consultation with the Board direct.

## Section 11

Staff of Service

(1) Subject to article 195 of the Constitution the Board may in consultation with the Public Services Commission engage the staff necessary for the proper and efficient performance of its functions.

(2) The Board may on the recommendation of the Government Statistician engage the services of consultants and experts as are necessary for the performance of its functions.

(3) The staff, consultants and experts shall be engaged on the terms and conditions that the Board may determine.

(4) The Board may delegate a power of appointment conferred on it by this section to the Government Statistician. } 12, Secretary to the Board

17\. Disclosure of information ' This Act does not affect a law relating to the disclosure or non-disclosure of an official, : secret or confidential information or trade secret. ‘ 18. Restriction on publication

(1) Subject to this section, except with the prior consent in writing of the person making the return or giving the answer or in the case of an undertaking, the owner for the time being of the undertaking, a person shall not publish, or show to any other person not employed in the performance of a function under this Act any of the following:

(a) an individual return or part of a return made for the purposes of this Act; ay

(b) an answer given to a question put for the purposes of this Act;

(c) a report, an abstract or any other document, containing particulars comprised in the return or an answer so arranged as to identify the particulars with a person or an undertaking.

(2) Subsection (1) does not apply to an information transmitted by the Government Statistician to the President, a Ministry, Department or any other Government organisation for the purpose of its functions.

(3) This section does not prevent or restrict the publication of a report, an abstract or any other document which would make identification of an undertaking possible merely by reason of the fact that the particulars relate to an undertaking which is the only undertaking within its particular sphere of activities, if the report, abstract or other document is so arranged as to disclose in respect of the undertaking, only the following information namely,

(a) the quantity and the value of a description of goods manufactured, produced, exported or sold;

(b) the number, the economic and social characteristic of employees employed;

(c) the amount and extent of investment; and

(d) any other information which has been furnished or supplied under this Act, to the publication of which the objection has not been made in writing by the person furnishing or supplying the information prior to the publication of the report, abstract or other document.

(4) This section does not require the disclosure to a Ministry, Department or any other Government organisation for the purposes of taxation, of an information acquired under this Act. ae 19.
