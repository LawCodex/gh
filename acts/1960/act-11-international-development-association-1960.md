(3) The sums of money received by the Government from the Association shall be paid into the Development Fund. 3. Personality of Association

(1) The Association is a body corporate with perpetual succession and may, subject to subsection (2) of this section, sue and be sued in its corporate name and may enter into contracts and, for and in connection with the purposes of the Agreement, may acquire and dispose of real and personal property.

(2) The President may, by legislative instrument, make Regulations for carrying into effect any of the provisions of the Agreement, relating to the status, immunities and privileges of the Association and of the governors, executive directors, alternates, officers and employees.

(3) A provision of this Act shall not be construed as limiting the power of the President under section 2 of the Diplomatic Immunities Act, 1962 (Act 148)’ to declare the Association as an organisation mentioned in that section or a power of the President under that or any other enactment to confer immunities or privileges on the Association, or on any other persons connected with it. 4, Interpretation In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires, } “Agreement” means the Articles of Agreement for the establishment and operation of an international body to be called the International Development Association; 2.
