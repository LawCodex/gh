(1) A person does not qualify to be elected to a unit committee unless that person |

(a) isa citizen of not less that eighteen years of age; :

(c) is ordinarily resident in the particular unit for which that person seeks elec- | tion; and |

(ad) has paid all taxes and rates or made arrangements satisfactory to the appropriate authority for the payment of the taxes and rates of that person. |

(2) For the purposes of paragraph (c) of subregulation (1), a person is ordinarily resi- ; dent in a unit if within the four years prior to the holding of the election to the unit, that person has lived in the area of the unit for an aggregate period of not less than twelve months. F

(3) A person does not qualify to be elected to a unit committee if that person

(a) is of unsound mind;

## Section 5

Qualification to vote | A voter registered in the unit is entitled to vote at the elections.

## Section 6

Mode of elections :

(1) The unit committee elections shall be held by secret ballot.

(2) Voting shall take place in all units on a day and at the places and times specified : by the Electoral Commission. :

(3) A voter shall not thumbprint on the ballot paper for more than ten candidates.

## Section 8

Candidate not to use political party symbols : A candidate seeking election to a unit committee

## Section 9

Penalty 7 A candidate who contravenes a provision of regulation 7 or 8 commits an offence and on summary conviction the Commission shall cancel the nomination of that candidate. '
