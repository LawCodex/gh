REPUBLIC OF GHANA

The One Thousand one hundred and twenty-eighth

ACT OF THE PARLIAMENT OF THE REPUBLIC OF GHANA ENTITLED EMISSIONS LEVY (REPEAL) ACT, 2025

AN ACT to repeal the Emissions Levy Act, 2023 (Act 1112) and to provide for related matters.

Date of Assent: 2*April, 2025. PASSED by Parliament and assented to by the President Act 1112 repealed 1. The Emissions Levy Act, 2023 (Act 1112) is repealed. B* B'

Savings 2. Despite the repeal of Act 1112, a right, liability or an obligation existence immediately before the repeal of Act 1112 shall continue existence until the right, liability or obligation is exercised or terminated.
