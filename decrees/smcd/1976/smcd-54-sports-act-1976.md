Membership

(1) The governing body of the National Authority is a Council consisting of

(1) The Council shall, as soon as possible after the expiration of each financial year, but within six months after the expiration of that financial year, submit to the Minister an annual report dealing generally with the activities and operations of the National Authority and the implementation generally of this Act within that year.

(a) information with regard to the proceedings of the Council and the policy of the National Authority and the proceedings of the Assembly within the year as the Council considers appropriate;
