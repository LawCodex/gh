ACT 996

THE NINE HUNDRED AND NINETY-SIXTH ACT OF THE PARLIAMENT OF THE REPUBLIC OF GHANA ENTITLED LUXURY VEHICLE LEVY (REPEAL) ACT, 2019 AN ACT to repeal the Luxury Vehicle Levy Act, 2018 (Act 969) and to provide for related matters. DATE OF ASSENT: 19th August, 2019. PASSED by Parliament and assented to by the President:

## Section 1 — Act 969 repealed

The Luxury Vehicle Levy Act, 2018 (Act 969) is repealed.

## Section 2 — Transitional provision

Despite the repeal of Act 969, a right, liability or an obligation in existence immediately before the repeal of Act 969 shall continue in existence until the right, liability or obligation is exercised or terminated. Date of Gazette Notification: 19th August, 2019.
