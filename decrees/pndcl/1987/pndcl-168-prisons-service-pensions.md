P.N.D.CL. 168 | PRISONS SERVICE (PENSIONS) ACT, 1987! | AN ACT te provide pensions te members of the Prisons Service and for related matters. 1. Disapplication of S.M.C.D. 8 to Prisons Service On the coming into force of this Act, the provisions of the Pensions and Social Security (Amendment) Decree, 1975 (S.M.C.D. 8), other than those provisions relating to the Pensions Act’, shall cease to be to members of the Prisons Service and a member of the : Prisons Service who before the commencement of this Act is required by virtue of the Pensions and Social Security (Amendment) Decree, 1975 (S.M.C.D. 8) to contribute towards the pension or to the social security fund as a member of that fund shall on the coming into force of this Act cease to make contribution towards that pension or to the social security fund. 2. Refund of accumulated contributions |

(1) Subject to subsection (2), the amounts paid by or on behalf of a member of the Prisons Service as contributions towards that member’s pension or social security fund before the commencement of this Act shall be refunded to that member

(a) — when the member retires from the Prisons Service on attaining the retirement age prescribed under the Prisons Service Decree, 1972 (N.R.C.D. 127);

(b) where the member resigns or is dismissed from the Prisons Service on at- . taining the age of superannuation in accordance with the provisions of the ; Social Security Decree, 1972 (N.R.C.D. 127).

(2) An amount of money payable to a member of the Prisons Service under subsection (1) shall be paid to the estate if that member dies before the member is due to retire from the Prisons Service or the retiring awards are paid or before attaining the age of superannuation. . i, This Act was issued as the Prison Service (Pensions) Law, 1987 (P.N.D.C.L. 168) made on the 13th day of j February, 1987, and nolified in the Gazette on 13th March, 1987. : 2.
