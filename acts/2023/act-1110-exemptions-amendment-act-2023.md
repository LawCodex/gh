DATE OF ASSENT: 29th December, 2023. PassED by Parliament and assented to by the President Headnote before section 17 amended 1. The Exemptions Act, 2022 (Act 1083), referred to in this Act as the “principal enactment”, is amended by the substitution for the headnote before section 17, of “Personal Effects, Foodstuffs, Equipment for Trial and Fishing Gear”

Exemptions (Amendment) Act, 2023 Act 1110 Section 19A imserted 2s The principal enactment is amended by the insertion after section 19, of “Fishing gear 19A. Fishing gear imported for agricultural purposes and
