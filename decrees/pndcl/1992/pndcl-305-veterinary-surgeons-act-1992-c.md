VETERINARY SURGEONS ACT, 1992! * AN ACT to establish a body to regulate the affairs of veterinary surgeons and to provide for related matters. The Veterinary Authority

## Section 1

Establishment of the Veterinary Authority

There is hereby established an Authority to be known as the Veterinary Authority.

## Section 2

The governing body BG

(1) The governing body of the Authority is a Council consisting of

(a) four registered members of the Ghana Veterinary Medical Association elected by the Ghana Veterinary Medical Association,

(b) the Director of Animal Health and Production Department of the Ministry of Agriculture,

(c) one representative of the Council for Scientific and Industrial Research nominated by the Director-General of the Council,

(d) one legal practitioner of not less than ten years standing as a lawyer nominated by the Government,

(e) one veterinary surgeon from one of the Universities in Ghana, and

() one distinguished and recognised livestock farmer.

(2) A registered veterinary surgeon elected by the members of the Council from among their number shall be the chairman of the Council.

(3) There shall be a vice-chairman of the Council who shall be elected by the members from among their number.

(4) The members of the Council shall be appointed by the President in accordance . with article 70 of the Constitution.

## Section 4

Tenure of office of members of the Council

(1) Members of the Council shall hold office for a period of three years and are eligible for re-appointment. i ) (2) A member of the Council may at any time in writing addressed to the chairman resign from office. |

(3) In the event of death of, or resignation from office by a member of the Council a new member shall, subject to subsection (5), be elected or appointed to hold office for the remaining term of office,

(4) Where the chairman certifies that a member of the Council is absent from Ghana or is in any manner unable to perform the functions of office, another person may be appointed or elected subject to subsection (5). ;

(5) An election or appointment of a member shall be made under subsection (4) if the remaining term of office is less than one year.

(6) A member of the Council who is a member by virtue of the profession or office shall cease to be a member when the member ceases to be a member of that profession or a holder of that office.

## Section 5

Meetings of the Council

(1) The Council shall meet at least three times in a year or at the shorter intervals determined by the chairman. |

(2) The chairman shall preside at the meetings of the Council and in the absence of the chairman the vice-chairman, and in the absence of both, the members present shall appoint one of their number to preside.

(3) Subject to subsection (2), the chairman and two other members of whom at least one is a veterinarian shall form a quorum.

(4) The decision at a meeting of the Council shall be determined by a majority of the

\- * members present and voting and in the event of an equality of votes, the chairman or the ° person presiding shall have a casting vote.

(5) The Council may co-opt a person to act as adviser at any of its meeting but the co-opted person shall not vote on a matter for decision by the Council. :

(6) The proceedings of the Council shall not be invalidated because of a vacancy in the membership or a defect in the appointment or qualification of a member, :

## Section 7

Financial provisions

(1) The funds of the Authority consist of

(a) __ fees, grants and gifts from both governmental and non-governmental sources, and po

(b) any other moneys and assets that may vest in or accrue to the Authority. .

(2) The moneys received by or on behalf of the Authority shall be deposited to the credit of the Authority at a bank approved by the Council.

(3) The Council may pay to the members, committee members and persons attending meetings of the Council the allowances determined by the Board with the approval of the Minister.

## Section 8

Accounts and audit

(1) The Authority shall keep proper books of account and other records relating to its funds,

(2) The books and accounts of the Authority shall be audited each year by the Auditor- General.

(3) The auditor shall forward a copy of the report to the Minister, who shall on receipt and after further discussions with the Board enforce the recommendations embodied in the report.

## Section 9

Report of the Authority

(1) The Registrar of the Authority shall annually submit to the Minister a report on the Authority’s activities for the year.

(2) A member may obtain a copy of the Authority’s annual report on the payment of a fee determined by the Council. Registration of Veterinary Surgeons : )

## Section 16

Temporary register

(1) A person who does not intend to practice permanently in Ghana but who is otherwise qualified to be registered in the Register is entitled to be registered in a temporary register as a veterinary practitioner.

(2) A person otherwise qualified to be registered in the Register and who is required

\* by the Council to serve a period of mandatory occasional training shall, on payment of the fee payable on registration, be registered in a temporary register.

(3) A person on a temporary register is entitled to be transferred to the standing register on producing to the Registrar the written evidence that the Council may require.

## Section 17

Restrictions of practitioners on temporary registers

(i) A practitioner on the temporary register shall not practise veterinary medicine in Ghana unless under a registered veterinarian of good standing.

(2) A practitioner who fails to comply with subsection (1) commits the offence of infamous conduct in a professional respect and shall be considered not to have been registered under this Act.

## Section 18

Publication of register

The Registrar shall once every year, publish in the Gazette a list containing the names of registered veterinary surgeons.

## Section 19

Evidence of registration )

(1) A copy of the list published in accordance with section 18 is evidence that the persons named in the list are registered under this Act.

(2) The absence of the name of a person from the list is evidence that that person is not so registered,

(3) The register, copies of it and extracts from it certified and signed personally by the Registrar shall be receivable in evidence by a court of law. \ [Issue 1] Vil — 3806

## Section 22

Procuring registration by false representation | A person who, for the purpose of procuring the registration personally or for another person under this Act, wilfully makes or produces or causes to be made or produced, a false certificate verbally or in writing and a person aiding or assisting in that act commits an offence and is liable on conviction to a fine not exceeding five hundred penalty units or in default to a term of imprisonment for two years or both the fine and the imprisonment.
