## Section 1

Nullification of Nana Kusi Apea I’s enstoolment The purported enstoolment of Nana Kusi Apea I as Omanhene and Paramount Chief of Wenchi is contrary to custom and is hereby declared to be null and void.

(2) Nana Mbore Bediatuo VI is therefore hereby restored as Omanhene and Paramount Chief of Wenchi with immediate effect.

## Section 3

Nana Kusi Apea I not to assume role of chief PG

(1) Nana Kusi Apea I shall not

(a) purport to be Omanhene or Paramount Chief of Wenchi whether under customary law or otherwise,

(b) in any manner assert any claim to be Omanhene or Paramount Chief of Wenchi, {c) do any act tending to incite disaffection against Nana Mbore Bediatuo VI, — Omanhene of Wenchi. ;

(2) Where Nana Kusi Apea I contravenes a provision of subsection (1) of this section he commits an offence and liable on conviction to 4 term of imprisonment not exceeding two years.

## Section 4

Nullification of Queen mother’s removal

(1) The purported destoolment of Nana Gyebua Ababio, the late Queenmother of Wenchi was contrary to custom and is hereby declared to be null and void.

(2) The late queenmother was and remained the properly enstooled queenmother until her death.

## Section 5

Removal of divisional chiefs nullified

(1) The purported destoolment of the following divisional chiefs namely, Nana Twum Ameyaw II, Kyidomhene; Nana Ankama Yaw Boamponsem, Adumhene; Nana Takyi Gyan, Gyasehene; Nana Kyereme Asiasaa, Twafohene and Nana Kofi Num, Gyassewahene was contrary to custom and is hereby declared to be null and void.

(2) The divisional chiefs are hereby restored to the traditional status they occupied before the enactment of the Chieftaincy (Amendment) Decree, 1966 (N.L.C.D. 112).

## Section 6

No person to recognise Nana Kusi Apea I:

(1) A person shall not

(a) recognise Nana Kusi Apea I as Omanhene and Paramount Chief of Wenchi whether under customary law or otherwise,

(b) im any manner support, encourage or canvass any claim of Nana Kusi Apea I d to be Omanhene or Paramount Chief of Wenchi,

(c) do any act tending to incite disaffection against Nana Mbore Bediatuo VI, Omanhene and Paramount Chief of Wenchi.

(2) A person who contravenes a provision of subsection (1) of this section commits an offence and is liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding five hundred penalty units or to a term of imprisonment not exceeding two years or to both the fine and the imprisonment. . 4 [Issue 1] VII — 4102
