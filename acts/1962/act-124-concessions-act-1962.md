## Section 14

Regulations

(1) The Minister may, by legislative instrument, make Regulations

(a) in respect of the records to be maintained for the purposes of this Act and for the imposition of fees for those purposes; i

(b) in respect of a matter relating to the Tribunal including the payment of fees and allowances to the members; and {c) for the purposes of giving effect to the principles and provisions of this Act.

(2) A statutory instrument made under the provisions of the Concessions Act 1939° and in force on the day immediately before the date of the commencement of this Act shall continue in force on and after that date as if the instrument was made under this Act.

## Section 15

Pending matters

(1) A matter relating to a concession referred to in section 2, and pending before a Court on the day immediately before the date of the commencement of this Act shall be continued and concluded on or after that date in accordance with the provisions of the Concessions Act, 1939.

(2) Where the matter is not concluded within twelve months from that date, the matter shall be deemed to have lapsed and the concession to which it relates shall be determined subject to the payment of the rent or royalty due up to the date of the lapse.
