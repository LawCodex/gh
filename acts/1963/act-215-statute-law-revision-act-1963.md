# Statute Law Revision Act, 1963 (Act 215) (Revised Edition)

STATUTE LAW REVISION ACT, 1963 (ACT 215) ARRANGEMENT OF SECTIONS Section 1. Repeal of obsolete, spent or unnecessary Enactments. 2. Consequential provisions relating to Enactments specified in Paragraph 1 of the Schedule. 3. Transfer of certain Moneys to the Consolidated Fund. 4. Savings. SCHEDULES First Schedule—Enactment repealed Second Schedule—Enactments referred to in section 4

ACT 215

THE TWO HUNDRED AND FIFTEENTH ACT OF THE PARLIAMENT OF THE REPUBLIC OF GHANA ENTITLED THE STATUTE LAW REVISION ACT, 1963 AN ACT to revise the statute law by repealing obsolete, spent or unnecessary enactments and for purposes connected therewith. DATE OF ASSENT: 10th December, 1963. BE IT ENACTED by the President and the National Assembly in this present Parliament assembled as follows—

## Section 1 — Repeal of Obsolete, Spent or Unnecessary Enactments

The enactments specified in the First Schedule to this Act (which enactments have become obsolete, spent or unnecessary) are hereby repealed.

## Section 2 — Consequential Provisions Relating to Enactments Specified in Paragraph 1 of the

Schedule.

(1) The share of the Government of Ghana in relation to any assets, liabilities or any property which, immediately before the coming into operation of this Act, were vested in any institute or council established by or under any of the repealed enactments specified in paragraph 1 of the First Schedule to this Act shall vest in the Ghana Academy of Sciences established by the Ghana Academy of Sciences Instrument, 1963 (E.I. 5).

(2) Subject to such directions as may be given by the President—

(a) all employees of any such institute or council shall, after the coming into operation of this Act, continue as employees of the Ghana Academy of Sciences so far as this may be practicable; and

(b) every contract subsisting between such institute or council and any other person immediately before the coming into operation of this Act shall be deemed to subsist between the Ghana Academy of Sciences and any such person.

(3) The provisions of this section shall be deemed to have come into operation on the first day of January, 1963.

## Section 3 — Transfer of Certain Moneys to the Consolidated Fund

Any moneys which, immediately before the coming into operation of this Act, remained in the trust fund established by the Agricultural and Horticultural Trust Fund Ordinance (Cap. 161) shall be transferred to the Consolidated Fund and shall form part of it.

## Section 4 — Savings

Notwithstanding the repeal of the Administration (Ashanti) Ordinance (Cap. 110) and the Administration (Northern Territories) Ordinance (Cap. 111), any statutory instrument made under either of the said Ordinances and in force immediately before the commencement of this Act shall continue in force as if made under the corresponding provision of either of the enactments specified in the Second Schedule to this Act.
