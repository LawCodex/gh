# Public Elections (Presidential and Parliamentary Election Date) Regulations, 1996 C.I. 14

CI. 14 PUBLIC ELECTIONS (PRESIDENTIAL AND PARLIAMENTARY ELECTION DATE) INSTRUMENT, 1996 ARRANGEMENT OF SECTIONS Section

## Section 1

Date of Presidential and Parliamentary Elections.

## Section 2

Voting.

## Section 3

Notification of Nomination Day.

IN exercise of the powers conferred on the Electoral Commission by Article 51 of the Constitution this Instrument is made this 12th day of June, 1996.

## Section 1 — Date of Presidential and Parliamentary Elections

The elections to the Office of President and for membership of Parliament shall be conducted by the Electoral Commission on Saturday, 7th December 1996.

## Section 2 — Voting

Voting shall take place throughout the country at the prescribed polling stations on the date specified in this Instrument.

## Section 3 — Notification of Nomination Day

The Electoral Commission shall by notification published in the Gazette, announcement on the national radio and television and publication through such other medium of mass communication as it considers appropriate, notify the public of the day appointed for the nomination of candidates for the elections. DR. K. AFARI-GYAN Chairman, Electoral Commission Date of Gazette Notification: 8th June, 1996.
