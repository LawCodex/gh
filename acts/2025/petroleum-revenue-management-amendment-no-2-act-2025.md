# Petroleum Revenue Management Amendment no.2 Act, 2025

Act 1155

REPUBLIC OF GHANA

The One Thousand one hundred and fifty-fifth

ACT OF THE PARLIAMENT OF THE REPUBLIC OF GHANA ENTITLED PETROLEUM REVENUE MANAGEMENT (AMENDMENT) (NO. 2) ACT, 2025

AN ACT to amend the Petroleum Revenue Management Act, 2011 (Act 815) to expand the definition of “qualifying instrument” to include other qualifying instruments prescribed by the Minister by Executive Instrument.

Date OF ASSENT:

December, 2025.

PASSED by Parliament and assented to by the President:

Section 61 of Act 815 amended

## Section 1

The Petroleum Revenue Management Act, 2011 (Act 815) is amended in section 61 by the substitution for the definition for “qualifying instrument”, of “qualifying instrument” means

(a) a debt instrument denominated in internationally convertible currency that bears interest or a fixed amount equivalent to interest

(i) that is of an investment grade security, and
