SOCIAL SECURITY (APPLICATION TO PUBLIC SERVANTS) ACT, 1973'

AN ACT to provide for the appointment to public officers of the Social Security |

Act, 1991 (P.N.D.C.L. 247) and for related matters. 1. Extension of Social Security Act, 1965

(1) Despite anything to the contrary, the Social Security Act, 1965 (Act 279), shall be deemed to have applied to all persons in the public service in respect of service rendered after the 31st day of December, 1971.

(2) Without prejudice to the generality of subsection (1), in respect of that service, | the terms “employer” and “worker”, as defined by section 30 of that Act’, shall, with effect from the ist day of January, 1972 be deemed to have included respectively, the Republic and a member of the public service. 2. Restriction of law relating to pensions

The service of a person in the public service after the 3ist day of December, 1971, shall not be pensionable service for the purposes of the laws relating to pensions and those laws shall be deemed to have ceased to apply to that person in respect of service after that date.
