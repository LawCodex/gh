CHIEFTAINCY (MEMBERSHIP OF REGIONAL HOUSES OF CHIEFS) INSTRUMENT, 2020 IN exercise of the powers conferred on the National House of Chiefs by section 6 of the Chieftaincy Act, 2008 (Act 759), this Instrument is made this 30th day of June, 2020. Regional Houses of Chiefs

## Regulation 1 — Membership of Regional Houses of Chiefs

(1) Membership of a Regional House of Chiefs shall not affect the status of a Chief and shall not be construed so as to confer paramountcy on any chief or to prejudice the claim of any chief to the status of a Chief.

(2) The membership of the Regional Houses of Chiefs shall be in reference to the Paramount Stool or Skin and not the individual occupant of the Stool or Skin.
