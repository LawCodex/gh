# Armed Forces (Amendment) (No.1) Regulations, 2000 C.I. 28

C.I. 28 ARMED FORCES (AMENDMENT) (NO.1) REGULATIONS, 2000 C.I 12 amended

## Section 1

The Armed Forces Regulations, 1970 (CI. 12) are amended as follows:

(a)

By the insertion after article 206. 45 of the following:

"206. 46-Retired pay and gratuity for commissioned and non-commissioned officers An officer of a previous non-commissioned rank appointed to a short service commission shall retire on pay and gratuity as applicable to a permanent regular officer, provided the total period of service is not less than fifteen years of which the period of continued service in the commissioned rank is ten years";

(b)

By the substitution for subparagraph (1) of article 206. 91 of the following:

"206. 91-Conditions relating to marriage To be eligible for pension a surviving spouse should have been married to the service personnel employee at the time of the employee's death"; and

(c)

By the insertion immediately after subparagraph (15) of article 206. 01 of

the following, "(16) "Spouse" includes widow and widower wherever it appears in the Armed Forces Regulation."

## Section 2

Article 206. 46 shall be deemed to have come into force on the 1st day of January, 1992. [Date of Gazette notification: 28th January, 1998] [Entry into force: 10th May, 2000]
