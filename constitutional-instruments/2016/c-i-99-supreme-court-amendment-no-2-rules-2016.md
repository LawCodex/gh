IN exercise of the power conferred on the Rules of Court Committee by clause (4) of article 33, clause (3) of article 64 and clause (2) of article 157 of the Constitution, these Rules are made this 12th day of October, 2016. Rule 68 of C. I. 16 amended

## Section 1

The Supreme Court Rules, 1996 (C.I. 16) referred to in this enactment as the principal enactment is amended in rule 68 by the addition of a new subrule (7) as follows:

“(7) A petitioner shall not amend a petition so as to add unto or alter the grounds of petition as stated in the filed petition.”.
