HOUSES OF CHIEFS) INSTRUMENT, 2013 IN exercise of the power conferred on the National House of Chiefs by section 6 of the Chieftaincy Act, 2008 (Act 759), this Instrument is made this 17th day of July, 2013. Membership of Regional House of Chiefs

## Section 1

Membership of a Regional House of Chiefs does not affect the status of a chief and shall not be construed to confer paramountcy on any chief or to prejudice the claim of a person to the status of a chief. Members of Ashanti Regional House of Chiefs
