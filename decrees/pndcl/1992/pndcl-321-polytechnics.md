# Polytechnics Act 1992, PNDCL 321 (Revised Edition)

P.N.D.C.L. 321 Polytechnics Act, 1992 P.N.D.C.L. 321 _ POLYTECHNICS ACT, 1992' . 1. The Act was originally issued as the Polytechnics Law, 1992 (P.N.D.C.L. 321) made on 5th January, 1993 : and nolified in the Gazette on Sth March, 1993. This Act was repealed by the Polytechnic Act, 2007 (Act 745). VI -315la [Issue 2]
