# Second Session of Parliament (Commencement) Instrument, 1993. C.I.5 (Revised Edition)

C.L5 Second Session of Parliament (Commencement) Instrument, 1993 SECOND SESSION OF PARLIAMENT (COMMENCEMENT) INSTRUMENT, 1993

[CL 5] : | [Article 112) (16th November, 1993)] | KNOW YE ALL MEN that in the exercise of the powers conferred on the Speaker by : clause (1) of article 112 of the Constitution, I DANIEL FRANCIS ANNAN, Speaker of Parliament, by this Constitutional Instrument, appoint the Accra International Conference Centre as Parliament House to be the place and ten o’clock in the morning of Thursday, the 6th day of January, 1994, to be the time at which the Second Session of the | First Parliament of the Fourth Republic of Ghana shall commence. : [Date of Gazette notification: 19th November, 1993] | [Spent.'} | 1. But published as an example of the type of Instrument that the Speaker issues for the beginning of a session of Parliament. The Constitution does not make provision for the Prorogation of Parliament. | [Issue 1] 1-292 :
