## Section 3

LI, 495 revived. N.L.C.D. 49 RENTS STABILISATION ACTS (REPEAL) DECREE, 1966! AN ACT to repeal certain laws and to provide for related matters. | 1. Repeal of Acts 109 and 168 The following enactments are hereby repealed:

(a) the Rents (Stabilisation) Act, 1962 (Act 109), and

(b) the Rents (Stabilisation) (Amendment) Act, 1963 (Act 168). 2. Restoration of customary rules of land tenancy

(1) A rule or any other requirement of customary law relating to the tenancy of land which was revoked, amended or in any way affected by or under the Acts repealed by section 1, shall, on and after the coming into force of this Act, have and be given the same effect as it had and was given under customary law immediately before the coming into force of the repealed Acts.

(2) This Act shall not affect or be deemed to affect the previous operation of the repealed Acts or the rights accruing to, or liabilities including obligations incurred by, a person by or under the repealed Acts before the coming into force of this Act. oo 3.
