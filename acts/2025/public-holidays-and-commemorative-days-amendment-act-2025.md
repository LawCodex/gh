Public Holidays and Commemorative Days Act 1142 declare any other day to be observed as a public holiday instead of that day.

(2) Where a public holiday provided under section 1 is to be observed on a Tuesday, a Wednesday or a Thursday, the President may, by executive instrument, declare the public holiday to be observed on the Friday immediately following that Tuesday, Wednesday or Thursday.

(3) Where a public holiday provided under section 1 falls on a Saturday or a Sunday, the President may, by executive instrument, declare the public holiday to be observed on the Monday immediately after that Saturday or Sunday.

(4) Subsection (2) does not apply to the observance of the following days as public holidays:
