# Second Session of Parliament (commencement) Instrument, 2002.C.I.35. (Revised Edition)

C.L. 35 Second Session of Parliament (Commencement) Instrument, 2002 | SECOND SESSION OF PARLIAMENT | (COMMENCEMENT) INSTRUMENT, 2002 [(Article 112) (4th January, 2002)] : KNOW YE ALL MEN that in the exercise of the powers conferred on the Speaker of Parliament by Article 112 of the Constitution, I, PETER ALA ADJETEY, Speaker of Parliai ment, do by this Constitutional Instrument appoint Parliament House, Accra, to be the place and ten (10) o’clock in the forenoon of Tuesday, the 15th day of January, 2002, to be the time at which the First Sitting of the Second Session of the Third Parliament of the —— Fourth Republic shall commence. ( Given under my hand this the 4th day of January, 2002. PETER ALA ADJETEY Speaker Spent {Date of Gazette notification: 4th January, 2002] ™~ I — 507 [Issue 1]
