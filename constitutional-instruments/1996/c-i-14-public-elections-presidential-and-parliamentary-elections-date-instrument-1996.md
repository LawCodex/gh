# Public Elections (Presidential and Parliamentary Elections Date ) Instrument, 1996 C.I. 14 ( Revised Edition )

C.1.14 Public Elections (Presidential and Parliamentary Elections Date) Instrument, 1996 PUBLIC ELECTIONS (PRESIDENTIAL AND PARLIAMENTARY ELECTIONS DATE) INSTRUMENT, 1996 vom [CL 14] . [(Article 51) (12th June, 1996)] |

## Section 1

Date of Presidential and Parliamentary elections The elections to the office of President and for membership of Parliament shall be conducted by the Electoral Commission on Saturday, the 7th December, 1996. ; 2. Voting CG Voting shall take place throughout the country at the prescribed polling stations on the date specified in this Instrument. |

## Section 3

Notification of nomination day

The Electoral Commission shall, by notification published in the Gazette, announce-

ment on the national radio and television and publication through any other medium of mass communication as it considers appropriate, notify the public of the day appointed |

for the nomination of candidates for the elections. [Spent] i [Date of Gazette notification: 28th June, 1996] I— 349 {Issue 1]
