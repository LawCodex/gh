# Electronic Communications (Amendment) Act, 2019 (Act 1006)

ACT 1006

ELECTRONIC COMMUNICATIONS (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2019 (ACT 1006) ARRANGEMENT OF SECTION Section

## Section 1

Section 25B inserted THE ONE THOUSAND AND SIXTH ACT OF THE PARLIAMENT OF THE REPUBLIC OF GHANA ENTITLED ELECTRONIC COMMUNICATIONS (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2019 AN ACT to amend the Electronic Communications Act, 2008 (Act 775) to exempt incoming intra-community roaming traffic within the Economic Community of West African States (ECOWAS) region from the minimum rate for international incoming electronic communication traffic and for related matters. DATE OF ASSENT: 28th December, 2019. PASSED by Parliament and assented to by the President

## Section 1 — Section 25B inserted

The Electronic Communications Act, 2008 (Act 775) is amended by the insertion after section 25A of section 25B as follows:— "Exemption from minimum rate for international incoming traffic 25B. (1) Despite subsection (1) of section 25A, incoming intracommunity roaming traffic within the Economic Community of West African States (ECOWAS) region is exempt from the minimum rate for international incoming electronic communication traffic.

(2) The Authority shall issue directives periodically to determine the rate for incoming intracommunity roaming traffic within the Economic Community of West African States (ECOWAS) region in accordance with Regulation C/REG. 21/12/17 on Roaming on Public

Mobile Communications Networks in the ECOWAS Region or any other Regulation in force; and on related matters.". Date of Gazette Notification: 30th December, 2019.
