# Third Session of Parliament (Commencement) Instrument, 1994 C.I. 9

C.I. 9 THIRD SESSION OF PARLIAMENT (COMMENCEMENT) INSTRUMENT, 1994 KNOW YE ALL MEN that in exercise of the powers conferred on the Speaker by clause (1) of article 112 of the Constitution, I DANIEL FRANCIS ANNAN, Speaker of Parliament, by this Constitutional Instrument, appoint the Accra International Conference Centre as Parliament House to be the place and ten o'clock in the morning of Friday, the 6th day of January, 1995, to be the time at which the Third Session of the First Parliament of the Fourth Republic of Ghana shall commence. Given under my hand this 16th day of November, 1994. JUSTICE D. F. ANNAN Speaker Date of Gazette Notification: 25th November, 1994.
