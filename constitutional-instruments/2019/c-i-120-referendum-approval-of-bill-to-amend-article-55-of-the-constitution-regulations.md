REFERENDUM (APPROVAL OF BILL TO AMEND ARTICLE 55 OF THE CONSTITUTION) REGULATIONS, 2019 IN exercise of the power conferred on the Electoral Commission by Article 51 of the Constitution, these Regulations are made this 3rd day of July, 2019. Preliminary Provisions

Purpose of Regulations :

### Section 1

The purpose of these Regulations is to regulate the conduct of a Referendum by the Electoral Commission to approve the Bill to amend clause 3 of article 55 of the Constitution to allow political parties to sponsor candidates for election to District Assemblies or lower local government units. Polling stations

### Section 2

For the purpose of the Referendum, the Electoral Commission shall conduct polls in all the designated polling stations throughout the country. Referendum Centre

### Section 3

(1) The Commission may designate the head office of the Commission for use as a Referendum Centre.

(2) The Commission shall, at least fourteen days before the date fixed for the Referendum, publish in the Gazette the place designated as a Referendum Centre.

(3) The Referendum Centre shall be the centre for the collation of the national results for the Referendum.

Register of voters

### Section 4

The register of voters of all the polling stations in the country shall

be the register for voting in the Referendum.

Qualification for voting at the Referendum

### Section 5

A person is qualified to vote at the Referendum if that person is

(a) a registered voter; and .

(b) not disqualified by any law for the time being in force from voting at public elections and referenda.
