# Financial Administration (Amendment) Act, 2008 (Act 760)

ACT 760

FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2008 (ACT 760) ARRANGEMENT OF SECTIONS Section 1. Arrangement of sections amended 2. Section 66 of Act 654 amended 3. Section 67 of Act 654 amended 4. Section 68 of Act 654 amended 5. Section 69 of Act 654 amended 6. Section 70 of Act 654 amended 7. Section 74 of Act 654 amended THE SEVEN HUNDRED AND SIXTIETH ACT OF THE PARLIAMENT OF THE REPUBLIC OF GHANA ENTITLED FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2008 AN ACT to amend the Financial Administration Act, 2003 (Act 654) to provide for a Financial Administration Court and to provide for related matters. DATE OF ASSENT: 2nd July, 2008. ENACTED by the President and Parliament

## Section 1 — Arrangement of sections amended

The arrangement of sections of the Financial Administration Act, 2003 (Act 654) is amended by the substitution of "Court" for "Tribunal" in the heading of Part VIII.

## Section 2 — Section 66 of Act 654 amended

Section 66 (1) of Act 654 is hereby deleted.

## Section 3 — Section 67 of Act 654 amended

Section 67 of Act 654 is amended by the substitution for "Tribunal" of "Court" and wherever "Tribunal" appears in the Act.

## Section 4 — Section 68 of Act 654 deleted

Section 68 of Act 654 is hereby deleted.

## Section 5 — Section 69 of Act 654 amended

Section 69 of Act 654 is amended by the substitution for "Tribunal" of "Court" wherever it appears.

## Section 6 — Section 70 of Act 654 amended

Section 70 of Act 654 is hereby deleted.

## Section 7 — Section 74 of Act 654 amended

Section 74 of Act 654 is amended by the insertion of the definition of "Court" after the definition of "Controller and Accountant-General" "Court" means the Financial Administration Court." Date of Gazette Notification: 4th July, 2008.
