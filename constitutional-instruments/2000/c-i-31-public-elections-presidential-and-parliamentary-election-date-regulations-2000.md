# Public Elections (Presidential and Parliamentary Election Date) Regulations, 2000 C.I. 31

C.I. 31 PUBLIC ELECTIONS (PRESIDENTIAL AND PARLIAMENTARY ELECTION DATE) INSTRUMENT, 2000 ARRANGEMENT OF SECTIONS Section

## Section 1

Date of Presidential and Parliamentary Elections.

## Section 2

Voting. IN exercise of the powers conferred on the Electoral Commission by Article 51 of the Constitution this Instrument is made this 22nd day of August, 2000.

## Section 1 — Date of Presidential and Parliamentary Elections

The elections to the Office of President and for membership of Parliament shall be conducted by the Electoral Commission on Thursday, 7th December, 2000.

## Section 2 — Voting

Voting shall take place throughout the country at the prescribed polling stations on the date specified in this Instrument. DR. K. AFARI-GYAN Chairman, Electoral Commission Date of Gazette Notification: 22nd September, 2000.
